# Procurement memo: week of 2026-10-21

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Forward-buy durum wheat and flour.** Cover about 40% of the next 3 months' needs this week, in tranches. Owner: head of procurement. Accelerate if weekly wheat prices keep rising above 1.2% for 3 weeks. Items: A0127. Path: farmers are holding back grain after a 3-month decline, so domestic wheat prices are likely to keep rising slowly.
2. **Packaging film: short tenor.** Keep film contracts to 3 months or link them to a resin price index. No 12-month fixed prices. Owner: packaging buyer. Items: A0727. Path: new Asian polymer capacity may ease resin prices, and a fixed long term would lock in above-market cost.
3. **Partial FX hedge.** Hedge about 50% of the next 3 months' FX-linked film and imported input purchases with forwards. Owner: treasury with procurement. Items: A0618, A0606, A1342. Path: the ruble rose from about 87/USD in September to 82.89 on 21 October, and some economists call this temporary. Hedging half keeps upside if the rate holds.
4. **Diesel index in truck contracts.** At each carrier renewal, price freight off a published diesel index instead of a fixed rate. Owner: logistics. Items: A0574, A1220. Path: the partial diesel export lift is contested (FAS says no domestic price rise, Reuters says Russia lacks fuel for export), so fixed rates leave us exposed if diesel tightens.
5. **QR-code audit on Kazakhstan lanes.** Confirm every outbound truck consignment carries the required QR-code data, and get carriers to confirm in writing before the next dispatch. Owner: logistics and customs. Items: A0826. Path: Tyumen customs turned back 713 trucks entering from Kazakhstan over QR-code gaps. The item does not say whether the rule applies to our outbound batches, so we verify rather than assume.

## 2. Monitor

- **Rail and truck capacity, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan lanes** (A0865, A1556). Southern port throughput has nearly stopped, and cargo is shifting to the north-west and land crossings. Trigger: freight rates up more than 10%, or crossing waits rising for 2 weeks. Then book a secondary carrier.
- **Italian strike and pasta supply** (A1580, A0578, A1369, A1922, A1229). Trigger: the strike lasts beyond 2 weeks and our export buyers in Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan or China report substitute shortages. Then offer supply to those buyers.
- **Domestic diesel price** (A0574). Trigger: a monthly rise above 2%, or the regional fuel price agreements lapsing. Then bring forward the surcharge review under decision 4.
