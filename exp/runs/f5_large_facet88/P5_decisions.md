# COO Decision Memo: Week of 18-21 October 2026

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Enforce QR-code verification on Russia-bound trucks at Kazakhstan exits.** Tyumen customs turned back 713 trucks from Kazakhstan for missing QR-code compliance on goods lots [A0826]. Our trucks heading to Russian clients risk the same rejection and return cost. We will brief carriers, require QR documentation checks before dispatch, and add a return-leg cost line to Russia-bound quotes.

2. **Update driver and client entry guidance for the QazETA system.** Kazakhstan has sent clarifications on its electronic entry permits to all 85 visa-free states, Russia included [A1408]. We will issue updated instructions to Russian-national drivers and to Russian clients' staff, so no crew is refused at the border.

3. **Add dual-use screening for drone-related cargo on China-origin shipments.** China's export controls on certain drones and autonomous-flight, radio and navigation technologies take effect 18 November [A0285]. Before that date we will screen China-origin consignments for these items and require exporter licences where needed.

4. **Tighten counterparty screening.** The US sanctioned the law firm Karnakis and Karnakis and five linked individuals for supporting Iranian procurement [A1728]. The EU is preparing a 22nd package targeting shadow-fleet vessels and energy and banking figures [A0765]. We will rescreen clients, consignees, and vessel or carrier names against these lists, and hold shipments with any match until cleared.

5. **Prepare, but do not commit, contingency rail capacity.** Russian Azov-Black Sea ports have halted after attacks, costing an estimated 200 bn rubles a month [A1556]. Importers may shift volume to rail routes through Kazakhstan. We will ask carriers for indicative wagon and slot availability and hold pricing options.

## 2. Monitor

- **A0765 and A1728:** exact scope and timing of the 22nd EU package and any US designations touching our client base.
- **A0285:** final product list and licensing procedure before 18 November.
- **A1556:** port restart timeline and any diversion of Russian importers' cargo to rail.
- **A1408:** whether QazETA rules change for transit or commercial drivers.
- **A0596:** whether the EAEU reports on a possible Armenian exit lead to new customs or tariff measures affecting Caspian or Eurasian transit.

## 3. Ignored as not relevant

A0414, A0133, A0895. These concern the Arctic Northern Sea Route, EU-Russia trade volumes, and a Russia-China gas pipeline, none of which touch our rail and truck corridors in the near term.
