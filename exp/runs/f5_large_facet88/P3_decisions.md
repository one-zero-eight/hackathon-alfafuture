# Decision memo: news review, week of 19-21 October 2026
From: General Counsel
Scope: items A0768, A0293, A0443, A0559, A0853, A1825, A0347, A1331, A0486, A1547

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Review any agency or pickup-point (PVZ) arrangements before 1 December.** Ozon will charge PVZ owners up to 1% of revenue for agent services from 1 December (A0293). If we use PVZ partners, or sell through marketplace logistics that rely on agency contracts, we need to check fee pass-through and contract terms. Owner: Legal and Ops.

2. **Start an evidence file on Ozon promotion terms, without filing a complaint yet.** Sellers say new Ozon algorithms remove their ability to cap discounted volume and to exit sales (A0443). We sell our brands on Ozon, so we need to know whether we are affected. Record our own promo settings and any forced terms. Decide on FAS engagement after reviewing the evidence, and possibly through a trade association.

3. **Audit advertising claims and consumer-protection practices.** A New Mexico court fined Meta $25bn for deceptive practices (A1825). The case is US law and has no direct effect here, but it is a warning on consumer-deception risk in our advertising. Run an internal check of ad claims, pricing displays and urgency messaging.

4. **Prepare, but do not yet adopt, a position on the platform-economy law.** The seller union asks for changes to fees and balance in the law (A0768). Our company is both a platform and a seller, so the position could cut either way. Draft options and hold until the bill's text is public.

## 2. Monitor

- **A0768**: possible amendments to the platform-economy law, which could change platform fee rules for our marketplace.
- **A0443**: FAS review of Ozon promotion terms. An outcome could set a standard for promo rules we rely on.
- **A0293**: whether Ozon's PVZ fee starts on 1 December as announced.
- **A0853**: Ozon's rising share of large-seller sales after the Wildberries disruption (A0853). This is a channel-concentration and continuity risk. Refer to the business team.
- **A0486**: Wildberries accepting state social certificates in five regions. Possible channel opportunity for our own brands; no legal action.

A0559, A0347, A1331, A1547
