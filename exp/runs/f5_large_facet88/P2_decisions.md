# Decision memo: Procurement & Supply Chain, week of 2026-10-21

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Audit QR-code paperwork on every Kazakhstan-bound consignment before dispatch.** Brief carriers and hold any truck whose lot lacks compliant QR documentation. Driver: [A0826] Tyumen customs turned back 713 trucks at the Kazakhstan border for QR non-compliance. Each rejected truck means a return trip, lost delivery slots and unpaid rail or truck time.

2. **Stagger forward purchases of durum wheat and flour.** Buy a portion of the next-quarter requirement now, not the full volume. Driver: [A0127] Wheat prices rose 0.4–1.2% a week after a three-month decline, and the trend is expected to continue. Our durum and flour costs will likely rise gradually.

3. **Hedge part of packaging film, not all of it.** Cover a modest share of near-term film needs while the ruble is strong. Driver: [A0618], [A0574] The ruble has strengthened to about 82.9/USD and 93.0/EUR from about 87/USD in September, though experts disagree on whether this lasts. Film is likely priced in hard currency, so a strong ruble lowers its cost now. Hold back on long-term fixed-price film contracts because [A0727] Chinese polymer capacity growth may lower film input prices later.

4. **Secure rail and truck capacity on northwest and land routes.** If any inbound durum or flour moves through Azov or Black Sea ports, reroute or pre-book alternatives. Driver: [A0865] Port handling in the south has nearly stopped, and cargo has shifted to the Northwest and land crossings, which may tighten capacity there.

5. **Keep current diesel surcharge terms with carriers.** Do not pre-buy fuel. Driver: [A0574] The FAS expects no domestic diesel price rise from the partial export-restriction easing, and regional price agreements are in place in 44 regions.

## 2. Monitor

- [A0618], [A0574]: ruble exchange rate. Revisit the film hedge if the ruble reverses.
- [A0127]: weekly wheat price trend. Add purchase volume if the rise accelerates.
- [A0826]: customs QR enforcement at the Kazakhstan border, including whether other customs posts tighten.
- [A0865]: southern port recovery and rail capacity in the Northwest.
- [A0727]: polymer prices in China, as a signal for the film contract timing.
- [A0575]: utility tariff indexation (household-focused). Watch only for spillover into industrial energy costs.
