# Decision Memo: Unrest in France (HR and Corporate Security)

Date: 2026-10-22

Context: Since 17-18 October, France has seen nationwide protests that turned into riots, arson, looting and clashes with police (A0137, A0376). A new national wave, "Act 2", is called for Saturday 24 October, with Paris preparing for new demonstrations (A1935, A0616).

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Store safety alert for 24 October.** Review all 140 stores, prioritising Paris region and the big cities where riots occurred. Confirm boarding-up and shutter plans. Stores in the most exposed areas may close early or stay closed on Saturday. Drivers: A0137, A1935, A0616. Why: looting and arson hit central shopping streets, and our stores are exposed in the same cities.
2. **No mandatory Saturday shifts in affected areas.** Staffing is voluntary, with a minimum crew only where the store is open. Drivers: A0616 (metro reported closed, burnt vehicles on streets). Why: staff commuting is unsafe and unreliable.
3. **Staff transport support.** Offer taxi or ride-share reimbursement for late or early shifts. Shorten shifts ending after dusk. Drivers: A0616, A0376 (curfew in some regions). Why: curfews and closed transport affect getting home.
4. **Stop-work right.** Staff may leave or not come in if they judge a site unsafe, with no sanction. Drivers: A0137 (hundreds of police injured, thousands detained). Why: protects staff from direct exposure.
5. **Travel advisory.** Suspend non-essential business travel to Paris and affected cities until 26 October. Drivers: A0137, A0551. Why: duty-of-care for travelling staff.
6. **Employee communication.** Send a short bilingual (French and Russian) briefing to all staff today: safety rules, contacts, and the stop-work right. Drivers: A0616 (Russian-speaking staff in Paris affected). Why: clear information reduces panic.
7. **Labour relations.** Make no pay or policy announcements in reaction to the protests. Protest drivers are cost of living and fuel prices (A0376). Do not sanction lawful participation outside working hours. Why: avoids inflaming tension.

## 2. Monitor

- **A1935**: possible ban on unauthorised demonstrations and changed police tactics. Could restrict store access.
- **A0376**: possible state of emergency and curfews. Could force closures.
- **A0616**: Act 2 scale and whether it spreads beyond Paris.
- **A0551**: spread to Milan and Madrid. Relevant to European stores.
- **A1745**: school-student protests over detentions. Youth mobilisation could grow.
- **A1620**: detention capacity overflow in five cities. Signals large-scale arrests.
- **A1579**: political escalation (Le Pen says state is losing control).
