# COO decision memo: week of 20-22 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Shorten validity and index RUB/CNY quotes.** The ruble has strengthened four days running, the CBR set USD at 81.97 for 22 Oct (lowest since 7 Aug), and CNY sits near 12.2-12.3 RUB (A1570, A1342, A0606, A1327). Russian importers' buying power is rising, but FX moves can erode our fixed-price margins. Action: cut quote validity to 5 days and add a clause indexing RUB and CNY rates to the official CBR fixing at payment.
2. **Tighten sanctions screening on energy-related cargo for European clients.** The EU will keep energy sanctions on Russia until the conflict ends, despite the US easing (A1170). Action: require end-use and origin documentation from European importers before accepting any energy-linked cargo, and re-screen the client book this week.
3. **Prepare Black Sea contingency.** Tankers and dry cargo are being hit near Turkey, Bulgaria and Romania (A0900). Our Middle Corridor and Russia-bound flows may use Black Sea legs. Action: request war-risk insurance quotes, pre-price a rail/Caspian alternative, and add force-majeure and war-risk surcharge clauses to new contracts.
4. **Review fuel contracts.** Oil prices are described as high (A1570), and EU fuel prices rose 31.4% y/y in September (A1072). Action: move diesel purchases to shorter fixed-price periods and add an oil-index adjustment clause to carrier contracts. The EU figure is a signal, not a direct cost for our Kazakh trucking.

## 2. Monitor

- **A0900**: Black Sea attack frequency and insurer reaction; trigger contingency in item 3 if Black Sea legs are affected.
- **A1170**: divergence between US easing and EU sanctions; any new EU measures on transit or re-export.
- **A0596**: EAEU stance on a possible Armenian exit. Kazakhstan co-authors the report, so customs and transit rules within the EAEU may shift.
- **A1570 / A1342**: sustained ruble strength and oil prices; retest pricing after the next CBR fixing.

## 3. Ignored as not relevant

- **A0414, A0530**: EU Arctic and Northern Sea Route ambitions. Our routes are China-Kazakhstan-Russia rail/truck and the Caspian/Middle Corridor, not the NSR.
