# Decision memo: General Counsel (week to 2026-10-21)

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Review our own promo and seller-terms against the Ozon complaint.** Driver: A0443. Sellers say Ozon's new promo terms removed their ability to cap discounted quantities and to pull items from sales, and the complaint names FBO sellers as most exposed. We run a marketplace and sell our own brands on Ozon and Wildberries, so we check our promo rules for the same two issues before any change. Owner: compliance.
2. **Prepare a platform-fee and seller-rights position paper.** Driver: A0768. A seller union has asked the presidential administration, FAS and the Duma economic committee to amend the platform-economy law, citing fees for placing goods on marketplace storefronts. As a platform operator we are in scope. We prepare our position now so we can respond to any draft amendment quickly.
3. **Check fee bases in our agency and partner-pickup contracts.** Driver: A0293. Ozon will charge pickup-point owners up to 1% of revenue from 1 December, justified as payment for agent services under the agency contract. We review whether our own agency and pickup-point contracts have a comparable fee basis and documentation.
4. **Emergency PHP patch and incident check.** Driver: A1730. Two critical PHP flaws were reported on 21 October, and experts warn that tens of thousands of Runet sites may be exposed. We confirm patch status across our PHP services and check whether any exposure would trigger personal-data breach notification duties.
5. **Business-continuity clause review.** Driver: A0421. The trade association has asked the government for an emergency trading mode during state-system outages. We check our customer terms and carrier and payment agreements for outage and force-majeure handling, and we support the association's request through industry channels.

## 2. Monitor

- **A0768**: whether a draft amendment to the platform-economy law appears.
- **A0443**: whether FAS opens a review of Ozon's promo terms.
- **A0293**: whether other marketplaces copy the pickup-point fee.
- **A1730**: any Russian-regulator advisory or confirmed exploitation.
- **A0421**: any government response to the outage-mode proposal.
- **A1825**: US precedent on large consumer-protection penalties for deceptive practices, relevant to our advertising claims.
- **A1076**: liability for quoted content, relevant to user reviews and seller content on our platform.

## 3. Ignored as not relevant

A0299, A1623, A0112.
