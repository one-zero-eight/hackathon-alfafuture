# GC memo: week of 21 October 2026

Themes: (1) platform-seller law and fee pressure; (2) marketplace promo control and FAS complaints; (3) mandatory marking scope expansion from 1 Nov; (4) manipulative dark patterns; (5) PHP vulnerability and brand-impersonation fraud; (6) exchange and fuel items; (7) Meta/US and other foreign items.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Marking scope (1 Nov):** Within 7 days, issue a seller notice and add a listing check for the newly covered categories (A1801). Block listings without valid marking for those categories from 1 Nov. Owner: head of seller compliance. Transmission: scope widens on a fixed date, so the Rule 2 lead time of 30-60 days has already passed. Direction check: scope expands, permanent. Effective date is 1 Nov, so act now.

2. **Promo control audit:** Within 14 days, audit our own promotion and discount terms for sellers, including who sets discount quantity and who can remove items from sales. Document the selection logic. Do not change these terms until the audit is done (A0443, cross-signal d). Owner: GC with commercial. Transmission: sellers say they lost quantity control and de-listing on Ozon, which may draw a FAS probe. Direction check: complaints rise, so freeze contentious changes.

3. **Platform-law comment package:** Within 2 weeks, prepare a position paper on placement fees and seller rights, and join the sellers' association and e-commerce association letters (A0768, A0421). Do not file contract changes until a draft bill exists (Rule 1). Owner: GC with public affairs. Direction check: sellers want fees limited, which would cut our commission revenue, so our exposure is as platform operator.

4. **Dark-pattern audit:** Within 30 days, review countdown timers, stock counters and "only N left" widgets on our site and in seller listings (A1035). Remove or re-label those that are not factual. Owner: product lead with GC. Direction check: consumer groups push for tighter rules, but no enacted law is cited, so audit and do not change contracts yet.

5. **PHP patch check:** Within 72 hours, confirm PHP versions on all sites and seller-facing tools, and patch any affected version (A1730, A1674). Prepare the 24-72 hour breach reporting protocol (Rule 3). Owner: CISO with GC. Transmission: an unpatched site could expose customer data. Direction check: no breach is reported for us.

6. **Brand-impersonation takedown:** Start weekly scans for lookalike domains using our brands, with a takedown trigger at any confirmed fraud site. Owner: brand protection with GC. Transmission: fraud using Russian brands is rising (A0762). Direction check: fraud increases, so this is a reversible, low-cost step.

7. **Seller notice on counterfeit liability:** Review the notice-and-takedown clauses in seller terms within 30 days, using the rule for counterfeits and seller content (Rule 7). Owner: GC. Direction check: no new court ruling is cited in these items, so this is a review, not a change in terms.
