# COO memo: week of 2026-10-21 (China-KZ-RU rail/truck, Caspian/Middle Corridor)

## 1. Decisions now

1. **QR-code audit on all Russia-bound truck consignments via Tyumen/Kurgan.** Check QR-code presentation for every batch before dispatch; hold any batch that fails. Owner: compliance, before next dispatch. Driver: A0826 (713 trucks turned back at the KZ border in 9 months for QR non-compliance). Path: refused entry means empty return legs and lost revenue.

2. **Diesel exposure review.** Review fuel-surcharge clauses in carrier contracts and decide a hedge share for next quarter. Owner: fuel/carrier procurement, this week. Driver: A0453 (Brent above $111, highest since May). Use diesel prices, not crude, as the trigger. Path: fuel cost is a direct margin hit on fixed-rate contracts.

3. **Cargo screening for drone-related HS codes before 18 Nov.** Screen for drones, autonomous-flight, radio-control and navigation items, and check clients who ship them. Owner: compliance, complete before the rule takes effect. Driver: A0285 (China export licensing from 18 Nov). Path: unlicensed shipments can be stopped or penalised.

4. **Surcharge review on China-to-Russia legs.** Price a border-delay surcharge based on measured dwell time at the KZ crossing. Owner: pricing, within two weeks. Driver: A0123 (cost rise from China-KZ border difficulties). Path: higher dwell time raises our cost per trip and cash tied up.

## 2. Monitor

- **Diesel exchange rules (A1547).** Trigger: a decree or exchange rule that limits participant access or caps diesel price moves. Then review fuel supply and carrier pricing.
- **Port diversion to land crossings (A0865, A0126).** Trigger: our rail or truck slot waits on the KZ-RU corridor rising for two consecutive weeks. Then add capacity and pre-book slots.
- **Caspian security (A0300).** Trigger: an attack on Caspian shipping or ports, or a rise in war-risk insurance. Then activate an alternative route plan.
- **US sanctions freeze (A0037).** Trigger: the freeze is lifted or Rubio sets a new measure. Then re-screen European and Russian importers.
- **EU 22nd package (A0765).** Trigger: formal adoption with bank or shadow-fleet restrictions. Then re-screen payment channels and counterparties.

## 3. Deliberately not acting on

- **A0765 Zakharova quote.** Rhetoric, and no measure has been adopted. Do not change operations because of it.
- **A0037 as a reason to ease screening.** The freeze is temporary and reversible. Keep full screening and do not reprice contracts on it.
- **A0126 coal to Baltic ports.** Bulk coal on a different network. Only act if our own wagon availability falls.
- **A0453 as a sole hedge trigger.** Crude moves do not equal diesel moves. Act on the diesel decision in item 2, not on the crude headline.
