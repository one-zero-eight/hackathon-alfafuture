# CFO memo: week of 21-22 October 2026

Themes: (1) ruble strength vs yuan; (2) CBR hold and no cut before 2027; (3) marketplace share shift; (4) payment channels (no friction); (5) summit headlines and unrelated items.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Accelerate yuan purchases:** buy 40% of 1-3 month CNY needs by 25 Oct, rest in weekly tranches. Items A1342, A1570. Path: stronger ruble lowers CNY/RUB (official 12.37 on 21 Oct, 12.28 on 22 Oct). Check (rule 1, direction): ruble is strengthening, but oil and October tax-period demand are partly temporary, so accelerate partially, not fully.

2. **Reduce forward cover on new yuan purchases to about 50%** while the ruble is strong. Items A1570, A1342. Path: cheaper yuan now lowers hedge cost, so less cover is needed. Check (rule 1): hedge less only while strengthening holds; revisit at trigger M2.

3. **No fixed-rate debt commitments before the 23 Oct CBR meeting.** Item A1081. Path: rate decision drives loan pricing. Check (rule 5): meeting within 1-2 days; expected outcome is a hold with hawkish tone, which clears the direction.

4. **After 23 Oct, fix about 30% of ruble loans for 12+ months** if the statement stays hawkish. Items A1081, A1098, A1545. Path: no cut until 2027 keeps rates high. Check (rule 4, direction, not level): expectations are for rates to stay high, but falling inflation expectations (13.2%) and 0.05% weekly CPI argue against 50%.

5. **Confirm committed ruble credit lines with current banks by 31 Oct;** draw early only if rhetoric turns hawkish. Items A1081, A1060. Check (rule 4): drawing early costs interest, so draw only on a clear rate signal.

6. **Re-run landed-cost budget at CNY/RUB 12.28 and hold ruble list prices.** Book the cost gain as margin. Items A1342, A1545. Check (rule 11, both legs): the ruble gain may reverse, so do not cut prices.

7. **Rebalance replenishment about 10% toward Ozon** in the next order cycle, keeping Wildberries volumes flat. Items A0586, A0853. Path: large sellers are moving sales to Ozon (58.29% share in August) after drone strikes on Wildberries. Check (rule 8): no commission change, so reallocate volume only, no price change.

8. **Keep two active CNY bank channels;** confirm settlement capacity with the bank by 31 Oct. Item A1592. Check (rule 6): no friction now, so no channel change, only a capacity check.

## 2. Monitor

- **M1:** CBR statement on 23 Oct (A1081). Hawkish hold confirms decision 4. A cut signal means moving new debt to floating 3-6 month tenor (rule 3).
- **M2:** Official CNY/RUB back above 12.4 (ruble weakening after tax period). Stop accelerating and raise forward cover to 60% (rule 2).
- **M3:** Weekly CPI above 0.1%, or population expectations (A1098) rising above 13.2%. Raise fixed share to 50%.
- **M4:** Any bank sanction or correspondent delay on CNY payments (none in these items). Add a third bank and a 2-4 week settlement buffer (rule 6).
