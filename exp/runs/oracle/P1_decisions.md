# CFO Decision Memo: Week of 21 October 2026

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Yuan purchases: buy part of the next two months' volume now, hedge the rest.** The ruble has strengthened four sessions running and the yuan fell 0.97% on MOEX to 12.2255 (A1570). The official yuan rate was set at 12.28 for 22 October (A1342). Because A0618 calls the rally partly temporary (diesel deal, mixed expert views), I will not lock the full forecast. Action: pull forward about one-third of November needs and hedge about 50% of Q4 yuan exposure through CNY/RUB instruments on MOEX. Dollar and euro trading is not available on MOEX (A1570), so I will not use USD/RUB as a hedge proxy.
2. **Fix part of the ruble working-capital loan rate for 12 months.** The Bank of Russia is expected to hold its key rate on 23 October with a hawkish tone, and cuts are not expected before 2027 (A1081). I will fix about half of the drawn balance and keep the rest floating. I will not lock long tenors, since A1081 leaves no near-term cut to benefit from.
3. **Hold a larger cash buffer through the October tax period** (A1570 flags it). I will keep the buffer at a level that covers tax payments without drawing on the revolver.
4. **Payment channel: keep yuan settlement through current Russian banks.** A1592 confirms that Russia and China will support banks operating in each other's territory and keep the share of national-currency settlement high. I will open a backup yuan account at a second bank as a continuity measure, without moving volume yet.
5. **Pricing guidance for the commercial team:** small-appliance prices may fall modestly to defend volume. A0128 shows small-appliance units up 17.6% while value rose only 13%, and large-appliance value fell 0.6%. Guidance: hold list prices and use promotions first, and pass a limited part of the ruble gain to retail prices. Keep the margin floor unchanged.
