# CFO memo, 22 Oct 2026

Context: ruble strengthening (CNY/RUB 12.37 to 12.28 official, 12.2255 on MOEX, four straight days up; oil strong; October tax period ahead). CBR meets 23 Oct, expected hold with a harder signal. Inflation is cooling.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Buy yuan now: 50% of the 1-2 month needs, in tranches over 3 days (Treasury, from 22 Oct).** Items A1342, A1570, A0323, A0618. Path: stronger ruble means cheaper yuan. Tax-period ruble demand may fade after ~28 Oct, so the window is short. Direction check: ruble is strengthening, so buying is allowed (rule 1). Keep the rest unbought. A0618 asks "what next" and the answer is not in the cards, so the move may reverse. Hedge no more than 20% forward.

2. **Do not fix any debt before the 23 Oct CBR meeting. Decide on 24 Oct.** A1081, A1098, A1545, A0825. Path: Shokhin expects a hold with a harder signal and no cut before 2027. Inflation is cooling (weekly 0.05%, public expectations 13.2%). Direction check: the rate path is "high for longer, but cooling", so the signal is mixed. Rule 5 applies, so no long fixed terms before the meeting. After it: if the signal is hawkish, fix 30% at 6-12 months. If neutral or dovish, stay floating.

3. **Draw or confirm committed credit lines (about 1 month of purchases) now, with short tenor of 3-6 months.** A1081, A1588. Path: rates stay high into 2027, and OFZ market stress (A1588) may tighten bank liquidity. Direction check: not fixing the rate, only securing availability (pattern d). Owner: Treasury, this week.

4. **Open a second and third settlement channel, with a small test payment (about 5% of monthly volume) within 2 weeks.** A1592, A0800, A1071. Path: the bilateral commitment to keep banks open is good news, but A0800 mentions "external pressure". Rule 6 says to diversify and keep a yuan buffer. Direction check: the lead time is 1-3 weeks, so start now while it is calm. Keep a yuan cash buffer of 2 weeks of payments.

5. **Add a 2-week settlement buffer to payment scheduling, and send suppliers' payments through the new bank screening flow.** A1071. Path: from 1 March banks reject transfers from devices with malware, which can delay payments. Direction check: this is a permanent regime, effective in ~19 weeks. Action now is low cost: IT security audit of payment-approval devices by 30 Nov, owner CIO/Treasury.

6. **Bring forward the pre-buying for the sales season (Nov-Dec) so goods land 4-8 weeks ahead. Use today's strong ruble.** A1342, A1570, A1545. Path: combines the ruble (cheap yuan) with seasonal demand (rule 9, pattern b). Direction check: both legs of ruble cost = yuan price x CNY/RUB; the CNY/RUB leg favors us now. Cap by demand: A1545 shows cooling prices but no demand data, so keep the volume at the base plan, not above.

7. **Ask for Chinese export volumes and prices in supplier quotes to be fixed in yuan for 60 days, with no ruble or USD-linked clause.** A0800, A1342. Path: trade is almost fully in national currencies, so the exposure is yuan only. Direction check: both legs matter, and a USD clause would add a third leg. Owner: purchasing.

8. **Pricing guidance: hold ruble prices, do not cut as the ruble strengthens. Re-check on 5 Nov.** A1342, A1570. Path: the strong ruble gives a margin gain; it may be temporary. Direction check: a temporary ruble move should not drive price cuts, so bank the margin as a buffer. Owner: commercial director.

## 2. Monitor

- CNY/RUB above 12.60 on MOEX, or two straight days of weakening: raise the hedge to 40% and stop buying (A1570, A1342).
- CBR 23 Oct statement: hawkish language or any hint of a hike: fix 30-50% of debt (A1081).
- Oil falling from the mid-May-style highs (A0649): ruble support fades, buy more yuan at once.
- Weekly inflation back above 0.1% for 2 weeks (A1545).
- Any sign of bank-channel sanctions (A1592 mentions pressure): move to a full buffer.
- A1588, Minfin and OFZ market: if the yields jump, draw lines early.
