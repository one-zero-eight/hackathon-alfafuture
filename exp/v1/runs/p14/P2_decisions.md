# Procurement memo, 22 October 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Forward-buy 2 months of durum and flour on fixed-price contracts this week.** Items: A0127. Path: farmers are holding back grain, so spot supply tightens and input cost rises. Check: rule 1, rising price means buy early. Sized at 2 months, not 4-6, because cross-pattern (a) is not met: no export-curb change, and the ruble is strengthening (A0618, A1570).

2. **Keep packaging film on contracts of 1 month or less, and do not pre-buy imported film.** Items: A1570, A1342, A0727. Path: a stronger ruble makes imports cheaper, and new Chinese base-polymer capacity may soften prices. Check: rule 7 reversed (ruble strengthens, so buy later), and rule 2 (falling prices, so no lock at highs). Hold the buffer at 3-4 weeks.

3. **Add diesel-indexed fuel surcharge clauses to all carrier and rail-truck contracts, and renegotiate freight now.** Items: A0453, A1072, A0123. Path: fuel costs feed directly into freight. Check: rule 8 (fuel up), but domestic petrol fell 0.7% (A1469), so index the surcharge rather than lock a higher fixed rate.

4. **Book Kazakhstan and China rail slots 2-4 weeks ahead, and pre-ship the next two weeks of volume.** Items: A0826, A0123. Path: truck backlogs at the Kazakhstan border raise transit time and cost. Check: rule 5 (congestion means pre-ship; rail lead time 1-3 weeks). Use trucks only for urgent small lots.

5. **Verify QazETA entry documentation for every Kazakhstan-bound load and driver before dispatch; hold non-compliant loads.** Items: A1408. Path: new entry rules could block shipments at the border. Check: rule 6 (documentation first). The items do not say whether the rules are temporary, so treat them as in force.

6. **Raise the China shipment plan for the next 1-2 months.** Items: A0823, A0455. Path: Russia-China agri trade and SCO trade are growing fast. Check: rule 10 (demand surge). Together with decision 4 this is cross-pattern (b): pre-ship and reroute.

## 2. Monitor

- **A1342, A0323, A1570 (ruble):** if USD closes back above 83 rubles, reverse rule 7 and buy imported film for 3-6 months.
- **A0127 (wheat):** if weekly rises exceed 2% for two weeks, or export curbs are announced, extend forwards to 4 months.
- **A1408 (Kazakhstan rules):** if the rules become permanent or extend to rail, review the route plan.
- **A0126 (rail wagons):** if coal volumes shift to the Baltic and wagon shortages appear, book slots 4 weeks ahead.
- **A0574, A0906, A1220 (diesel deal):** if domestic diesel prices rise after partial export-ban relief, apply rule 3 to forwards.

## 3. Deliberately not acting on

- **A0661, A0453 (European gas, Brent):** these do not set our contract prices, and domestic energy is not hedged on them. Brent is already captured through decision 3.
- **A1922 (Italian pasta strikes):** Italy is not one of our destinations.
- **A0906, A1220 (diesel-deal price effects):** a Putin claim and a Reuters doubt, both unverified.
- **A1545, A1098 (weekly inflation 0.05%, expectations):** no price shock; no change to buffers.
- **A0285, A0474 (China drone and rare-earth controls):** no link to our inputs or products.
