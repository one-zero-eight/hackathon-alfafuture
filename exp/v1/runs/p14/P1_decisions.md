# CFO memo, 22 Oct 2026: yuan, debt, pricing, peak season

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Buy 30% of the next 2 months' yuan needs this week; cut forward cover on 1-3 month needs from about 60% to about 40%.** Owner: Treasury. Items A1342, A1570. Path: official USD 81.97 (first sub-82 since 7 Aug) and CNY 12.28, with the ruble up four days running, make yuan cheaper in rubles. Check: ruble strength is partly oil-driven (A1570) and may fade, so buy now but keep part of the book open. Rule 1, direction confirmed.

2. **Fix 30-40% of ruble loans after the CBR meeting on 23 Oct, extending tenor to about 12 months.** Owner: Treasury. Items A1081, A1098, A1060. Path: a hold with a hawkish tone is expected and rate cuts are not seen before 2027 (A1081), so the rate stays high longer. Check: direction is "stays high", so fix. Rule 5 is respected by waiting for the 23 Oct decision before committing to long fixed terms. Rule 4.

3. **Draw 20% of committed credit lines now on 3-month tenor, and pre-agree fixed terms with two banks for execution on 23 Oct.** Owner: Treasury. Items A1081, A1060. Path: same as item 2, with the cost of early drawing accepted as the price of a buffer. Check: short tenor, so rule 5 does not block it. Rule 4.

4. **Pre-buy New Year season stock: order 6-8 weeks of expected Dec-Jan sales from China this week, for delivery by mid-November.** Owner: Procurement, budget sign-off by CFO. Items A1941, A0128. Path: peak season is roughly 8 weeks out and shipping takes weeks. Appliance units rose 7.4% but value grew more slowly (A0128), so keep promotional depth limited. Check: lead time versus event date. Rule 9. Cross-signal: buying yuan now (item 1) and pre-buying stock (item 4) together lock in the stronger ruble for the peak.

5. **Re-model Ozon margins for the 1 Dec pickup-point fee and update pricing guidance before the next Ozon promo cycle.** Owner: Commercial. Items A0293, A0853, A0443. Path: marketplace fee change raises cost to sell on Ozon, and large sellers are shifting volume there (60% share). Check: the fee amount is cut off in the headline, so use Ozon's published terms rather than an estimate. Rule 8.

6. **Ask Legal and Customs to confirm by 28 Oct whether the 1 Nov labeling expansion (A1801) covers our SKUs and what it adds to landed cost.** Owner: Legal. Item A1801. Check: cost impact not yet quantified. Rule 7.

7. **Keep current bank and payment channels; no new channel yet.** Owner: CFO. Items A0800, A1592. Path: Russia and China commit to keep each other's banks open and national-currency settlement is near 100%, which lowers channel risk. Check: no present friction, so rule 6 is not triggered.

## 2. Monitor

- **Ruble reversal:** official USD back above 82 for three consecutive days, or ruble weakening after 23 Oct. Trigger rule 2: buy 30-60% of 1-3 month needs at once and lock forwards. Items A1342, A0649.
- **Diesel deal and oil:** Reuters doubts the deal cuts prices (A1220), and oil fell after a five-month high (A0649). A sharp oil drop would weaken the ruble. Same trigger as above. Items A1463, A0906.
- **CBR signal on 23 Oct:** if the regulator signals a cut, switch new borrowing to floating or 3-6 month tenor (rule 3). Items A1081, A1098.
- **Import duty on localized goods:** Alikhanov's proposal (A1120). If enacted, pull forward imports (rule 7). If it is a cut, defer.
- **Ozon and marketplace rules:** seller complaints about promotions (A0443) may lead to changes. Revisit margins if terms move. Item A0443.
