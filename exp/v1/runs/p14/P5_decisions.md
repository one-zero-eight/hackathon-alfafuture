# COO memo: week of 21 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Kazakh crossings (Khorgos/Altynkol): pre-book Russia-bound rail wagons 2-3 weeks ahead for committed volume; add a 3-day transit buffer and a 3% surcharge on road cargo into Russia.** Items A0123 (China-Russia goods dearer because of difficulties crossing the Kazakh border), A0826 (Tyumen customs turned back hundreds of trucks at the Kazakh border). Path: border friction adds time and cost to China-Russia flows. Check: cause and duration are not stated, so treat as temporary; review in 2 weeks.

2. **Estonia crossings: notify European and Russian clients now; from 25 Oct route Russia-bound trucks via Estonia only with a 1-2 day buffer, or use an alternative crossing.** Items A0958, A1559. Path: shorter Estonian-side hours (Narva-1, Koidula, Luhamaa) and holiday queues extend dwell time. Check: the one-sided Estonian restriction is described as persisting, so plan for it as lasting.

3. **Screening: hold China-origin drone and autonomous-navigation components until end-use documents are on file; clear pre-18 Nov shipments before the new licensing applies.** Items A0285. Add EU/US screening for military-linked cargo (A0373, A1170). Path: new Chinese export licensing covers drones and anti-jam navigation, and EU-linked cargo scrutiny is rising. Check: EU, US and Kazakh/EAEU regimes differ; apply the strictest.

4. **Clients and payments: keep prepayment as the default for Russia-bound clients; re-screen names against A1728 and A1762 designations and drop any hit.** Item A0037 (Trump's freeze of the "hell sanctions" law is described as temporary). Cross-signal (a): EU tightening plus new US designations. Check: no bank-specific item was found, so the US relief does not justify relaxing terms.

5. **Fuel: add a diesel-indexed fuel surcharge to all road and carrier pricing, reviewed monthly; move carrier contracts to monthly fuel indexing now.** Items A1072 (EU fuel prices +31.4% y/y in September), A0453 (Brent above $111, first time since 20 May), A1485 (record Russian crude freight rates). Check: A0906 and A0574 (Putin's claim of a 5-6% diesel price drop; FAS says no domestic rise) are unverified. An index that also falls protects us either way, so use it instead of a flat surcharge.

6. **Capacity: lock Q4 wagon and carrier capacity for Russia-bound volume 4-6 weeks ahead; raise rail rates 5% on eastern lanes.** Items A0123 (Q3 Russian freight demand +20%), A0823 (China-Russia agri trade up over a third), A0865 (Azov/Black Sea port losses above 200bn RUB a month, cargo moving to rail), A0126 (Baltic coal rail flows down up to 80% in October as wagons are redirected). Cross-signal (c): peak demand plus wagon pressure. Check: demand and wagon pressure are persistent, not temporary; capacity is scarce, so price up.

7. **Documents: check driver and crew entry papers against Kazakhstan's QazETA permit; update client notices and delay any non-compliant shipment.** Item A1408 (QazETA rules sent to all 85 visa-free states, including Russia). Check: the rule details are not in the items; rule 8 applies, so documentation first, then shipment.
