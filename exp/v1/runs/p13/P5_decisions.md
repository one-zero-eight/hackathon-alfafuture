# COO memo: news week 18–21 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Screen drone and dual-use cargo before 18 Nov.** Items: A0285, A0373. China's drone export controls (autonomous flight, radio and anti-jamming navigation tech) take effect 18 Nov. Hold UAV and navigation-tech shipments without end-use documents; refuse non-compliant ones before the date. Italian court ruling (A0373) supports requiring documented end-use for military-linked cargo. Owner: compliance. Check: tightening, permanent; Chinese rule governs China-origin goods, EU/US rules govern onward destinations, strictest applies.

2. **Re-screen Iran-linked counterparties this week; prepayment only.** Items: A1728, A1762, A0123. OFAC added Iran-related entities on 21 Oct; Turkey-Iran transit cost doubled (A0123). Screen all clients and transit parties against the new listings; require prepayment from any Iran-adjacent client; drop flagged ones. Check: US secondary-sanctions risk, tighter (rule 4).

3. **Lock Q4 wagon blocks 4–6 weeks ahead, with a peak surcharge.** Items: A0123, A0126. Russian freight demand rose 20% in Q3; Baltic-bound coal loading from southern rail rises sharply in October. Book November–December wagon blocks now; add a proposed 8–10% surcharge on new bookings. Check: demand up, capacity tight, so raise price (rule 7, pattern c).

4. **Pre-book alternative Kazakh crossings for China–Russia cargo.** Items: A0123. Delivery from China to Russia is slowed by Kazakh border crossing difficulties. Pre-book wagons 2–3 weeks ahead on alternative crossings for affected consignments; add a proposed 3–5 day transit buffer. Check: appears temporary; review in two weeks before making it permanent.

5. **Add an indexed fuel surcharge; renegotiate carrier fuel clauses.** Items: A0453, A1072, A0661. Brent passed $111 for the first time since May; EU fuel prices rose 31.4% y/y, third monthly rise in a row. Add an indexed fuel surcharge to all contracts; push carriers to index fuel; lock longer fuel terms where offered. Check: rising, so reprice (rule 5).

6. **Reprice all services and notify clients two weeks ahead.** Items: A0453, A0123, A0126. Fuel and rail tightness hit together. Apply the new tariff across contracts and give two weeks' written notice. Check: costs up on both fronts, pattern (d).

7. **Activate Black Sea contingency routes.** Items: A0865, A0900, A0239. Southern Russian port losses exceed 200bn rub a month and transshipment has largely stopped. Shift Russia-bound cargo planned through Black Sea ports to northern rail; hold Caspian capacity as an option, booked 2–4 weeks ahead; notify affected clients. Check: deteriorating, so keep the northern route and add buffer (rule 2). No Caspian improvement is reported, so no full shift.

8. **Update QazETA documents for drivers and clients.** Items: A1408. Astana sent QazETA entry rules to all 85 visa-free states, including Russia. Update crew, driver and client document checklists and brief Russian clients and carriers now. The item gives no effective date, so confirm one with the authorities before cut-over. Check: new entry requirement, applies to Russian nationals (rule 8).

## 2. Monitor

- **A0958, A1559:** Estonian posts (Narva-1, Koidula, Luhamaa) restricted from 25 Oct; cards cite passenger and vehicle limits at Ivangorod, Kuninchina Gora and Shumilkino. Trigger: any freight-truck restriction on Russia–Estonia crossings; then reroute EU-bound trucks.
- **A1450:** 133 trucks queued at Verkhny Lars, up 16 in an hour. Trigger: queue rising for two consecutive days; then move Georgia-transit cargo to rail.
- **A0455:** SCO trade settled mostly in national currencies. Trigger: tenge weakness (rule 9) or bank-channel restrictions.
