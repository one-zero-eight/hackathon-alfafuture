# Procurement memo, week of 21 October 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Forward-buy 2 months of durum and flour at fixed prices by 31 Oct, split across at least three suppliers; keep about 40% on spot.** Items A0127, A0618. Path: domestic wheat up 0.4–1.2% a week after three months of decline, growers holding back sales. Direction check: rising prices, so buy early (rule 1). Not a 4–6 month lock: no export curb or poor harvest in the cards, and the ruble is strengthening.

2. **Cut imported packaging film buffer to 3 weeks and defer new imported orders for up to 2 weeks.** Items A0323, A1342, A0606, A0618. Path: USD/RUB near 82 against about 87 in September makes imports cheaper. Direction check: ruble strengthening, so buy imported inputs later (rule 7). Temporary vs permanent: A0618 attributes the rally partly to a temporary diesel-deal effect, so the deferral is capped at 2 weeks.

3. **Book rail slots for China and Kazakhstan lanes 3 weeks ahead; pre-ship the next 2 weeks of volume now; use trucks only for urgent small lots.** Items A0123, A0455. Path: border difficulties with Kazakhstan raise China delivery cost during record Q3 freight demand. Check: rail lead time is 1–3 weeks, so booking now fits. Cross-signal (b): route disruption plus peak season means pre-ship and reroute.

4. **Raise the Central Asia and China export shipment plan by about 10% for November–December; confirm volumes with distributors this week.** Items A0455, A0123. Path: Russia–SCO trade grew 18% in the first half, and freight demand is up 20%. Check: demand surge in destination means raise the plan 1–2 months ahead (rule 10). Confirm before committing extra rail slots.

5. **Add an FX adjustment clause or USD/CNY pricing to export contracts for KZ, UZ and CN.** Items A0618, A1570, A0606. Path: a stronger ruble cuts ruble margin on finished-goods exports. Check: exports are less profitable when the ruble strengthens (rule 7 reverse). The clause is cheap and reversible and avoids locking at the wrong rate.

6. **Add a diesel-indexed fuel surcharge clause to carrier renewals; do not pay a surcharge yet.** Items A1547, A0574, A1469. Path: government is preparing limits on exchange diesel trade, FAS says partial diesel export relief will not raise domestic prices, and petrol fell 0.7%. Check: fuel cost is not up at the pump, so add the clause only (rule 8).

7. **Issue an RFQ to one alternate packaging film supplier, with no volume commitment.** Items A0727, A0453. Path: Chinese base-polymer capacity growth may ease polymer supply. Check: no film price spike in the cards, so no 3–6 month lock (rule 4 not triggered).

## 2. Monitor

- **USD/RUB** (A0323, A1342): below 80 for 5 sessions extends the deferral in decision 2; back above 87 releases imported film orders.
- **Domestic wheat price** (A0127): two weekly declines triggers rule 2, cut buffer to 3–4 weeks and stop new forwards.
- **Film quotes vs Brent $111 and TTF above €90** (A0453, A0661): a film quote rise of 5% or more triggers a 3-month packaging lock (rule 4).
- **Diesel exchange-trade rules** (A1547): a formal change in access rules triggers a freight renegotiation.
- **Central bank rates and inflation expectations** (A1060, A1098): a rate move raises forward financing cost, so review forward size.
