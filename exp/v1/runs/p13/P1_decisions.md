# CFO memo: week of 21 Oct 2026

Core read: the ruble is strengthening (CBR official USD 81.97 and CNY 12.28 for 22 Oct; MOEX CNY 12.2255 on 21 Oct, fourth straight day of ruble gains: A1342, A1570, A0618). Rates are not falling: the CBR meets 23 Oct and is expected to hold with a hawkish tone; a cut is not seen before 2027 (A1081, A0825). The rally is partly temporary (oil prices, October tax period per A1570; diesel deal per A0618).

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Accelerate yuan purchases: buy about 50% of the next 2 months of yuan needs this week.** Drivers: A1342, A1570. Path: a stronger ruble lowers the ruble cost of yuan. Check: ruble strengthening, so buying now is correct (rule 1). Owner: Treasury.
2. **Keep about 40% of the next 3 months of yuan needs forward-covered.** Drivers: A1570, A0649. Path: part of the rally is oil- and tax-driven, and Brent has already fallen from a five-month high. Check: temporary, so do not go fully unhedged (rule 2 check: waiting hurts if the ruble turns). Owner: Treasury.
3. **Pre-agree fixed-rate loan terms for 30-50% of debt; sign only after the 23 Oct CBR decision.** Drivers: A1081, A0825. Path: rates stay high for longer, so fixing is right, but a fix before a meeting in under 2 weeks is premature (rule 5). Check: expectation is "high for longer", not "falling" (rule 4). Owner: CFO.
4. **Draw committed ruble credit lines early, covering 30% of the next quarter's working-capital need.** Drivers: A1081. Path: secures liquidity before any tightening. Check: rate direction is up or flat (rule 4). Owner: Treasury.
5. **Qualify a second bank for yuan settlement within 2 weeks; add a 2-4 week settlement buffer.** Drivers: A0800, A1592. Path: Russia-China national-currency settlement is near 100%, and bank cooperation is affirmed, which supports a second channel at low cost. Check: no current friction, so this is a hedge, not a response (rule 6). Owner: Treasury.
6. **Check payment devices and online banking for malware before 1 March.** Drivers: A1071. Path: banks will reject transfers from infected devices, which could block supplier payments. Check: timing is 4+ months out, so low cost now. Owner: IT with Finance.
7. **Revise the landed-cost budget for the 1 Nov labeling expansion; hold pricing guidance until the cost is quantified.** Drivers: A1801 (headline only). Path: tighter marking adds compliance cost. Check: cost direction is up, so rule 7 applies; size is unknown. Owner: Operations with Commercial.

Cross-signal: items 1-3 combine a strengthening ruble with high rates. Buy yuan now, fix debt only after 23 Oct.

## 2. Monitor

- **Brent / oil prices (A0649, A0906):** if the ruble weakens two weeks running, buy 30-60% of 1-3 month needs now (rule 2).
- **CBR 23 Oct statement (A1081):** a cut signal switches new debt to floating 3-6 months (rule 3); a hawkish hold confirms item 3.
- **Weekly CPI and inflation expectations (A1545, A1098):** a rise reverses the demand view and triggers a cut in purchase budget (rule 8).
- **Drone export controls from 18 Nov (A0285):** act only if our supplier list includes drones or drone components.
