# Decision memo: civil unrest in France, 19-21 Oct 2026
Author: Head of HR and Corporate Security

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Raise store security to elevated for Saturday 24 Oct.** Plan early closure or reduced hours for stores in central Paris and in the other big cities with protest activity, decided store by store with local managers by 22 Oct. Drivers: A1935 (new protest wave set for 24 Oct, possible bans, hardline policing), A0137 (looting and arson last weekend), A0616 (the "Act 2" call with yellow vests). Why: stores were looted and our staff and property are exposed.
2. **Build rosters around curfews.** Shifts must end before curfew starts in affected zones. Drivers: A0510 (curfews in central Paris, including Place de la Republique), A1272 (Paris curfew extended a second time). Why: staff cannot safely get home after curfew, and stranded staff will raise labour complaints.
3. **Cover late travel and limit non-essential trips.** Offer taxi reimbursement for late shifts; defer non-essential store visits and business travel to affected cities. Drivers: A0616 (metro closed, burned cars), A1745 (large protest at a suburban Paris court). Why: transport disruption and spillover near our sites.
4. **Issue employee crisis communication by 23 Oct, in French.** Cover safety guidance, the right to stay away from unsafe areas without penalty, and a neutral stance on protest participation. Drivers: A0137, A1597 (student-led movement), A1745 (schoolchildren detained). Why: many of our staff are likely students or young part-time workers.
5. **No pay or policy change now.** Revisit after 24 Oct.

## 2. Monitor

- A1935, A0616: scale and outcome of the 24 Oct action, and any ban on unauthorised demonstrations that could affect store access.
- A1620: custody capacity in Paris, Lille, Marseille, Bordeaux and Toulouse. Full facilities signal continued mass arrests.
- A1745: further youth or school protests that could draw in our young staff.
- A0510, A1272: new curfew extensions or new cities.
- A0137, A1192: whether the student movement spreads to unions or the retail sector.

## 3. Ignored as not relevant

A0551 (Europe-wide speculation covering Italy and Spain, no direct effect on our French operations), A1192 (opinion on blame), A1597 (root-cause analysis, no operational change).
