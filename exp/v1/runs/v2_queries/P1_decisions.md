# CFO Decision Memo: Week of 19-22 October 2026

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Pull forward part of yuan purchases.** Official CNY rate fell from 12.39 (A0323, 19 Oct) to 12.28 (A1342, 22 Oct), about 0.9%, and the ruble has strengthened four days running (A1570). Buy about 30-40% of next month's yuan requirement now instead of on schedule. Do not convert the whole book, since the trend may reverse. Hedge the remainder with forwards, not open positions. Driver: A1342, A0323, A1570, A0606.

2. **Fix the cost of ruble working capital.** The key rate will not be cut before 2027 (A1081, a view from RSPP's Shokhin, not a central bank statement), and the 23 October meeting is expected to hold with a hawkish tone. Do not budget for cheaper refinancing in 2026. Negotiate fixed-rate or capped tranches on the working-capital line and extend tenors where the spread is acceptable. Driver: A1081.

3. **Hold consumer prices; cut promotional depth, not list prices.** The ruble's gain lowers landed cost on yuan-priced goods. Guidance to the commercial team: pass through at most a third of the cost gain, and use it to fund margin recovery and lower Ozon/Wildberries discounts. Driver: A1342, A1570, A0853.

4. **Shift marketplace allocation toward Ozon.** Large sellers' share on Ozon reached 60% by late summer after drone strikes disrupted Wildberries logistics (A0853). Move incremental stock and ad spend to Ozon and cap Wildberries replenishment until its logistics recover.

5. **No change to cash buffers.** The news does not justify reducing the buffer while rate cuts are deferred.

## 2. Monitor

- **A1081**: CBR decision on 23 October. A hawkish outcome confirms the fixed-rate plan in item 2.
- **A1342 / A1570 / A0323**: whether the CNY official rate keeps falling. Reverse the early purchases if it rebounds above about 12.4.
- **A0474**: Chinese rare-earth export limits could lengthen Chinese component lead times. Check our supplier lead times.
- **A0285**: China's drone export controls take effect 18 November. Relevant only if our range includes drone-related components.
- **A0293**: Ozon's pickup-point fee from 1 December. Relevant only if we own pickup points.

A0594 (Wildberries card discount for bank customers, not our channel or bank choice). A0606 (repeats the official-rate figures already covered by A1342 and A0323).
