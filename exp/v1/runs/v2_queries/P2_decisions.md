# Decision memo: procurement and logistics, week of 21 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Forward-buy part of flour and durum wheat needs for the next 2 months, in tranches.** Domestic wheat prices rose 0.4–1.2% in the last week after three months of decline, and farmers are holding back sales (A0127). The news covers wheat generally, not durum specifically, so we treat it as a directional signal for our grain costs. Staged buying limits the risk of locking in too early.
2. **Keep current carrier rates and fuel-surcharge terms; do not pre-accept a surcharge increase.** Petrol fell 0.7% week on week, with AI-92 down 0.8% (A1469, A1734). The diesel export deal is not expected to raise domestic diesel prices, according to the FAS (A0574). Add a monthly index-linked review clause to truck contracts instead of a fixed surcharge.
3. **Hold a secondary diesel supply source for road legs to Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and China.** Reuters analysts say Russia's export volumes are too small to lower prices and that Ukrainian strikes limit output (A1220). Novak says only surplus diesel will be exported (A1271). Domestic supply therefore stays tight enough to make a backup contract worth its cost. We are not raising fuel stock levels on this basis alone.

## 2. Monitor

- **A1220, A1271, A0896, A0906, A1156:** whether the diesel export volumes (300 kt initial, 500 kt in November) are actually shipped. The first tanker is due to leave port on 22 October (A0906). A slip, or any cut to the domestic supply that is exported, would raise diesel risk for our rail-and-truck routes.
- **A0574:** whether the 125 regional anti-price agreements in 44 regions hold. A breakdown would mean fuel price pressure on carriers in the Volga region.
- **A0127:** whether the wheat price rise continues. If it stalls, extend the forward-buy plan (action 1) rather than buying the full volume now.
- **A1469, A1734:** weekly Rosstat fuel prices. Use them to trigger the index review in action 2.

## 3. Ignored as not relevant

- **A0374:** Siberian power tariffs for miners. We buy energy in the Volga region, and the item concerns grid capacity in Siberia.
- **A1734:** duplicates A1469 (same Rosstat weekly fuel data), so it adds no new decision input.
