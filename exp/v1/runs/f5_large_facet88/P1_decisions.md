# CFO Decision Memo: Week of 20-22 October

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Partially hedge upcoming yuan payments.** Lock forward cover on about half of the next 3 months of yuan supplier payables. Drivers: [A1342], [A1570]. The official yuan rate was cut to 12.28 and MOEX yuan fell 0.97% to 12.2255 on a ruble rally. A stronger ruble lowers the ruble cost of yuan purchases now, but the ruble has rallied four days in a row, so the move may reverse. Hedging protects against that reversal.

2. **Hold discretionary spot yuan buying until the trend is clearer.** Drivers: [A1342], [A1570]. Buy the yuan needed for committed orders as scheduled, and defer optional buying for a few days.

3. **Keep ruble debt structure conservative.** Drivers: [A1081], [A1098]. The central bank is expected to hold the key rate on 23 October, with no cut before 2027. Refinance floating-rate ruble loans into fixed rates where the spread is acceptable, and do not add new floating-rate borrowing. Hold a larger cash buffer for working capital.

4. **Hold consumer pricing; no broad price increases.** Driver: [A1545]. Weekly inflation slowed to 0.05%, and year-to-date inflation is 6.15%. Pass through supplier-cost changes only on affected SKUs.

5. **Shift marketplace budget toward Ozon.** Driver: [A0586]. Ozon's share of large sellers' marketplace sales rose to about 58%. Reallocate part of the marketing budget and inventory placement toward Ozon, while keeping Wildberries active.

6. **Confirm yuan payment channels with banks.** Driver: [A1592]. Russia and China agreed to support banks operating in each other's territory and to keep settlements in national currencies. Ask our banks to confirm continuity of yuan payment channels and fees.

## 2. Monitor

- [A1081] and the 23 October central bank decision: confirms or changes the no-cut view and the timing of ruble debt refinancing.
- [A1570] and [A1342]: whether the ruble rally holds. This determines the hedge ratio.
- [A1592]: concrete changes to bank or payment rules for yuan settlement.
- [A1071]: from 1 March, banks will reject transfers from devices with malware. Check our payment-approval devices and supplier payment flows before then.
- [A0449]: a possible geopolitical thaw could strengthen the ruble further and affect hedge timing.
