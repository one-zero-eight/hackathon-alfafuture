# Memo: French protest wave, decisions as of 22 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Cut hours at stores inside curfew zones and Act 2 routes.** Paris curfew, extended in some districts (A1504, A1873); "Act 2" protests on Saturday 24 Oct (A1935, A0616). Close or shorten hours 23-25 Oct at Paris-region stores in curfew communes or near announced routes. Reopen when the prefecture clears the area. Owner: Store Operations Director. Path: curfews cut trading hours and staff access. Check: direction is restrictive, dates are short and temporary, and scope is affected stores only, not all 140.

2. **Board up and protect stock in cities with recent attacks.** Paris, Lille, Marseille, Lyon (A0050, A0225, A1935). Move high-value lines to back rooms and board windows by 23 Oct, 18:00. Owner: Corporate Security. Path: looting and vandalism of shops on 17-18 Oct (A0225) repeat at the Act 2 protests. Check: pre-emptive and reversible; this is pattern (d), riots plus prior vandalism.

3. **Brief staff within 24h and adjust rotas.** SMS and email on 22-23 Oct, updates every few hours over the weekend. Where the metro is closed (A0616), allow flexible or later starts and pay for transport on late shifts. Shorten evening shifts under curfew. Owner: HR Operations. Check: disruption is local and days long, so use temporary rota changes, not contract changes.

4. **Add guards and panic buttons, 23-26 Oct.** Cover stores in Paris and large cities that stay open, including opening and closing. Owner: Corporate Security. Path: looting risk (A0225). Check: safety over sales; applies only to open stores.

5. **Defer non-essential staff travel into Paris and affected cities, 23-26 Oct.** Basis: a foreign embassy advisory to avoid protest sites (A0587) and the curfews. Source is not the French foreign ministry, so verify a French advisory before extending. Owner: HR Mobility. Check: temporary and limited to non-essential trips.

6. **Open early dialogue with unions and the CSE on cost of living.** Protests cite living costs and fuel prices (A0376); yellow vests join Act 2 (A0050, A0616). Hold a CSE information session before 24 Oct and prepare contingency staffing. Owner: Labor Relations. Check: no pay reform or SMIC change appears in the items, so no permanent contract change or formal consultation is triggered now. Modest raise only if talks stall (rule 7).

7. **Log looting losses and shrink** from 17 Oct onward, for insurance and police complaints. Owner: Loss Prevention. Basis: looted goods are being resold, and the government plans penalties (A0367). Check: cheap and reversible.
