# CFO memo: week of 21 Oct 2026

**Themes:** (1) ruble strengthening on high oil, yuan cheaper in rubles (A1342, A1570); (2) oil momentum fading, so ruble support may reverse (A0649, A0896, A1469); (3) key-rate path unclear ahead of the CBR meeting on 23 Oct (A1081, A1098); (4) China payment channels stable (A1592, A0800); (5) Chinese drone export controls from 18 Nov (A0285).

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Buy 40% of 1-3 month yuan needs this week; do not wait.** Drivers A1342, A1570. Path: CBR yuan rate fell 12.37 to 12.28 RUB (A1342), so each yuan costs fewer rubles. Check (playbook rule 1, ruble strengthening): buy now, hedge less. Passes.

2. **Lock forwards on 30% of the 3-month yuan need, not more.** Drivers A0649, A0896. Path: oil prices have fallen from their mid-October peak, and the ruble rally is oil-driven, so it can reverse. Check (rule 2, falling export revenue): waiting now carries reversal risk. Passes.

3. **Recompute landed-cost budget at 12.28 CNY/RUB; hold the price list for 2 weeks.** Drivers A1342, A1570. Path: cost = yuan price x CNY/RUB, so the ruble leg is cheaper. Check (rule 11, both legs): the yuan leg is unchanged, so take the saving as margin on Ozon/Wildberries rather than cutting prices while the rally may reverse.

4. **Keep ruble debt floating or 3-month tenor until after 23 Oct; no fixing before the meeting.** Drivers A1081, A1098. Check (rule 5, CBR within 2 weeks): the signals conflict. Inflation expectations fell to 13.2% (dovish), but Shokhin expects no cut before 2027 and a hold with a tougher tone. Do not fix.

5. **After 23 Oct, if the CBR holds with a hawkish tone, fix 30-40% of ruble debt for 12+ months.** Drivers A1081, A1098. Check (rule 4, rates stay high longer): direction is "high for longer" only if the hold is confirmed. Conditional, not automatic.

6. **Qualify a second yuan payment bank within 3 weeks; keep a 2-4 week settlement buffer.** Drivers A1592, A0800. Path: continuity of banks in both countries and near-100% national-currency settlement lower channel risk, but the pact is a declaration of intent. Check (rule 6, lead time 1-3 weeks): no current friction, so this is cheap insurance, not a trigger for prepayment.

7. **Screen the SKU list for drones and autonomous-flight items by 1 Nov.** Driver A0285. Path: new Chinese licences apply from 18 Nov, and a supplier may stop shipping or need a permit. Check (rule 6, supplier channel): cheap, reversible, time-bound. Act now.
