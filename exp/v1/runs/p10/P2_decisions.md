# Procurement memo, week of 2026-10-22

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Forward-buy 2 months of durum and flour (about 50% of monthly need) at fixed price; qualify one more domestic mill.** Owner: grain buyer, this week. Items: A0127 (wheat up 0.4–1.2% weekly after a three-month fall; farmers holding back). Path: domestic wheat cost rising. Check: rising price means buy early (rule 1). Size is 2 months, not 4, because the rise is small and no shortage or export curb is reported.

2. **Do not forward-buy imported packaging film; hold 3–4 weeks of cover and buy spot.** Owner: packaging buyer. Items: A0618, A0323, A0606, A0311 (USD near 82–83, lowest since August; ruble strengthened). Path: stronger ruble lowers import cost. Check: ruble strengthens means reverse (rule 7). Experts disagree on whether the move is temporary (A0618), so no 3–6 month lock.

3. **Add fuel-indexation clauses to all rail and truck contracts at renewal, and consolidate loads to full trucks.** Owner: logistics, within 4 weeks. Items: A0453 (Brent above $111, a five-month high), A1072 (EU fuel up 31.4% year on year), A0123 (freight demand up 20% in Q3). Path: fuel and freight cost up. Check: fuel up means add surcharge clause and renegotiate freight (rule 8). The clause is reversible.

4. **Book rail slots for China and Kazakhstan lots 3–4 weeks ahead; pre-ship the next 2 weeks of China-bound goods.** Owner: export logistics, this week. Items: A0123 (China deliveries costlier because of difficult Kazakhstan border crossings). Path: border slowdown delays rail. Check: disruption means pre-ship and reroute (rule 5). Rail lead time is 1–3 weeks, so 3–4 weeks covers it. Combines with peak freight demand (pattern b).

5. **Qualify a second packaging film supplier to 15–20% share within 6 weeks; no lock on it.** Owner: packaging buyer. Items: A0453, A1072 (energy spike), A0727 (Russian plastic exporters' niche may shrink, possibly freeing domestic polymer). Path: energy raises polymer cost. Check: energy spike means alternate supplier and check indexation (rule 4). The lock is deferred because the ruble move offsets it (decision 2).

6. **Set a truck contingency for Kazakhstan and China: if rail slot wait exceeds 3 weeks, move 30% of lots to truck.** Owner: logistics, pre-agreed this week. Items: A0123. Path: rail congestion. Check: reroute or switch carrier (rule 5). Truck is for urgent small lots only.

## 2. Monitor

- **Wheat price (A0127):** two consecutive weeks above 1.2%, or any export-curb news, means extend forwards to 4 months (rule 1).
- **Ruble (A0618, A0606):** USD back above 87, the September level, means buy imported packaging early (rule 7).
- **Diesel exchange rules (A1547, A0349):** a decree restricting diesel exchange access or price bands means review carrier fuel surcharges (rule 8).
- **Brent (A0453, A0649):** above $111 for two weeks means lock 3 months of energy-linked packaging (rule 4).
- **Plastics exports (A0727):** confirmed export limits means reprice domestic film.
