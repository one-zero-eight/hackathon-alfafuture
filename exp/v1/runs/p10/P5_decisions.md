# COO memo: news week of 18-21 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Lock Q4 wagons on China-Kazakhstan-Russia rail.** Book wagons 4-6 weeks ahead for November-December, with a 15% reserve over current bookings. Add a peak surcharge for Russia-bound rail. Items: A0126, A0123. Path: October coal diversion raises loading on the Kuybyshevskaya (+42%) and Yuzhno-Uralskaya (+66%) railways, and Russian Q3 freight demand rose 20%, so wagon shortage and rate pressure are likely. Playbook 6 and 7; cross-signal (c). Check: the October spike is coal-specific, so lock capacity only through peak, with no multi-year commitment. Direction: demand and costs up, so raise prices. Owner: rail booking desk.

2. **Add a border-delay surcharge on China-Russia rail.** Charge a surcharge and add 2-3 transit days on shipments crossing China-Kazakhstan points. Pre-book wagons 2-3 weeks ahead, and move urgent cargo to trucks. Item: A0123 (Chinese delivery cost up because of difficulty crossing the Kazakh border). Path: border friction raises cost and transit time. Playbook 1 and 11. Check: duration not stated, so treat as temporary and review the surcharge every two weeks. Owner: pricing lead.

3. **Add a diesel-indexed fuel surcharge.** Apply it to all truck and rail-trucking contracts, reset monthly, and open renegotiation with the main carriers. Items: A1072 (EU fuel prices +31.4% y/y, third monthly rise), A0453 (Brent above $111). Path: higher fuel raises carrier costs. Playbook 5. Check: prices are rising, so surcharge and renegotiate; do not lock long fuel contracts now. Owner: procurement.

4. **Screen dual-use drone cargo before 18 November.** Screen HS codes for UAVs and autonomous, radio and navigation components on China-origin cargo, and hold any shipment without end-use documents. Item: A0285. Path: China licensing applies to exports, which tightens what we can accept. Playbook 3. Check: the strictest applicable regime governs (China, EU, Kazakh), and the change is permanent. Owner: compliance.

5. **Tighten Iran-linked screening.** Run OFAC screening on counterparties and transit routes with an Iran nexus, and hold any Iran-linked cargo for review. Items: A1728, A1762. Path: US designations raise secondary-sanctions risk for non-US forwarders. Playbook 3 and 4. Check: US rules add to EU and Kazakh rules, so the strictest governs. Owner: compliance.

6. **Raise Black Sea alert; keep the northern route.** Warn clients with Black-Sea-exposed Europe-bound cargo of possible delay, and add buffer days. Do not move volume to the Middle Corridor yet. Items: A0900 (shipping attacks off Turkey, Bulgaria, Romania), A0123 (Turkey-Iran transit cost doubled). Playbook 2 and 10; cross-signal (b) is not met because no Caspian or Georgian improvement is reported. Owner: operations.

7. **Notify clients and crews about QazETA.** Update documents and crew entry requirements, and inform Russian clients and drivers before the next Kazakhstan crossing. Item: A1408 (permit rules sent to 85 visa-free states, including Russia). Playbook 8. Check: the rule covers foreign individuals entering Kazakhstan and is permanent. Owner: documentation.
