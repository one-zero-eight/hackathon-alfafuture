# HR and Corporate Security memo: French protest wave (week of 17 Oct)

Scope: the items cover Paris and other French cities. The items do not say which of our 140 stores sit inside curfew zones, so store-level mapping is the first step.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Saturday 24 Oct ("Act 2"): store closure rule.** Close or shorten hours (close by 17:00) in stores in Paris and any city with protests or curfews, by a decision taken by Store Safety on 23 Oct at 12:00. Owner: Head of Store Safety. Driver: A1935, A0605. Path: looting and damage risk to stock and fixtures.
2. **Curfew mapping.** Within 48 hours, map all 140 stores against the prefecture curfew zones (A1873, A0475, A0251). Owner: Store Safety with Store Operations. Any store inside a curfew zone closes before 22:30 until the curfew is lifted. Path: closing after 23:00 breaches a legal restriction and risks staff being stranded.
3. **Staff scheduling.** Cancel night shifts in curfew zones and move staff with commutes through affected areas (A0616: metro closed) to day hours. Offer paid flexibility to anyone who cannot travel safely. Owner: HR Scheduling. Path: people risk and absenteeism.
4. **Store protection.** Board up or shutter the highest-risk street-front stores in the affected cities by 23 Oct. Owner: Corporate Security. Driver: A0225 (shops looted, wider vandalism). Path: direct property loss and insurance exposure.
5. **Insurance check.** Confirm by 23 Oct that our policies cover riot and civil commotion, and set up damage photography for each incident. Owner: Finance and Legal. Driver: A0225, A0251. Path: claim eligibility.
6. **Employee communication.** Send a crisis notice to all staff on 22 Oct covering safety rules, the closure policy, and who to contact. Owner: HR Communications. Driver: A1935, A0616. Path: staff uncertainty.
7. **Resale monitoring.** Ask Loss Prevention to check whether our SKUs appear on resale platforms, using the report in A0367. Owner: Loss Prevention. Path: inventory theft signal.

## 2. Monitor

- **State of emergency (A0376):** if the government declares it, reassess all closure and curfew rules within 24 hours.
- **Police ban on unauthorized demonstrations (A1935):** if enacted, consider reopening hours for stores outside the affected zones.
- **Official curfew map:** when the promised map is published (A0251), redo the store mapping from item 2 the same day.
- **Sentinelle deployment (A0605, A0593):** military troops guard sites, not private stores, so do not count on it for our protection. Revisit only if we get an official protection commitment.

## 3. Deliberately not acting on

- **A0616:** one personal account. Anecdotal, not a source for decisions.
- **A0225 and A0605 political statements:** rhetoric and tactics, with no direct change to our rules.
- **A0367 resale crackdown:** a government request to platforms. It affects our recovery options, not our operations, so only item 7 applies.
- **A0616 mention of police guarding a brand's shop:** not relevant to us.
