# CFO memo: weekly news review (week of 17-21 Oct 2026)

Themes: (1) ruble strength and yuan cost, (2) rates and ruble debt, (3) sanctions and payment channels, (4) appliance demand and pricing, (5) Ozon marketplace terms, (6) southern port disruption.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Hedge yuan (Treasury, this week).** Forward-buy 40% of the next 90 days of yuan needs at today's levels (official CNY about 12.28 RUB, A1342). Buy the remaining 60% in three monthly tranches. Transmission: a stronger ruble lowers landed cost. Experts disagree on whether the move lasts (A0618, A1570), so do not lock 100%.
2. **Pass part of the FX gain to prices (Commercial, 2 weeks).** Pass about 50% of the landed-cost gain into channel prices. Hold the rest as a margin buffer until the ruble trend holds for two weeks. Driver: A1570, A1342, A0618.
3. **Volume in small appliances, no volume discounts in large appliances (Commercial).** Small appliances: units +17.6%, value +13% (A0128), so add entry-price SKUs. Large appliances: units +7.4%, value -0.6% (A0128), so no discounting to chase volume.
4. **Fix 40% of ruble working-capital loans for 12 months (CFO, before 23 Oct if bank quotes allow).** Transmission: the central bank is expected to hold rates, with no cut before 2027 (A1081). Fixing protects against that. Caveat: A1081 is one opinion, so the 23 Oct decision is the confirmation point.
5. **Prepare a second yuan payment channel (Treasury and Compliance, 30 days).** Open a backup channel through a Chinese partner bank, sized to one month of supplier payments. Keep the current route as primary. Drivers: A1592 (Russia-China commitment on bank operations and national-currency settlement) and A0037 (the US sanctions freeze is temporary).
6. **Re-route southern cargo (Logistics, this week).** Check whether any shipments in the next 3 months route through Novorossiysk, Taman or Tuapse (A0865). Move them to north-west or land routes, and add budget for demurrage and insurance.

## 2. Monitor

- **Yuan rate (A1342, A1570):** if the official CNY rate moves more than 5% from 12.28 in a month, re-size the hedge.
- **Central bank decision, 23 Oct (A1081):** if a cut is signalled, cap the fixed share at 40%. If a hike is signalled, raise it.
- **Ozon seller complaint (A0443):** if the antitrust service (FAS) opens a review, or Ozon changes promotion terms, move exposed SKUs out of promotions. Commercial to quantify exposure within 2 weeks.
- **US sanctions act (A0037):** if the freeze lifts or tariffs on China-origin goods return, review supplier pricing.
- **Ukraine talks in November (A1848):** if talks produce formal sanctions relief, reassess the backup channel only after the terms are published.
- **Port losses (A0865):** if southern ports resume regular cargo handling, end the re-routing.

## 3. Deliberately not acting on

- **A1848 (peace-talk headline):** no direct lever for us; speculative until terms are published.
- **A0037 (sanctions freeze):** the freeze is temporary and can be reversed, so no change to contracts or hedges.
- **A1081 (rate forecast):** one opinion from a non-central-bank source; we act on the central bank decision, not the forecast.
- **A0618 (ruble explanations):** experts disagree; no extension of the hedge beyond the plan above.
- **A1592 (Russia-China communique):** statement of intent only; wait for implementing rules before changing the primary channel.
