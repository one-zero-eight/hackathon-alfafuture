# Procurement & Supply Chain Memo (week of 2026-10-18)

Themes: (1) Kazakhstan border compliance; (2) rail and freight capacity and cost; (3) grain inputs and state support; (4) packaging polymers; (5) FX; (6) fuel exchange rules.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Verify QR-code compliance for every truck batch to Kazakhstan** before the next dispatch. Owner: logistics lead with customs broker. Driver: A0826 (713 trucks turned back at the Tyumen/Kurgan crossing for QR-code non-compliance). Transmission: refused trucks mean lost delivery time, demurrage and penalties on truck-route exports.
2. **Book rail wagon slots for November China and Kazakhstan shipments now**, covering at least the next six weeks of planned volume, with a written capacity commitment from the carrier. Owner: transport planner. Drivers: A0126 (rail capacity being redirected toward southern and Black Sea-bound freight), A0123 (cross-border transit costs rising, China deliveries costlier). Transmission: scarce wagons raise spot cost and push out shipping dates.
3. **Add an indexed transit-cost or surcharge clause to China and Kazakhstan freight contracts** at renewal, tied to a published rail tariff index. Owner: procurement. Driver: A0123. Transmission: protects margin on export pricing that was fixed before cost rises.
4. **Cover about one-third of next-quarter durum wheat needs forward**, not the full requirement. Owner: grain buyer. Driver: A0127 (wheat up 0.4–1.2% weekly, with sellers holding back). Transmission: input cost rises modestly, and a partial hedge limits exposure if the trend reverses.
5. **Prepare the eligibility file for the rail-freight subsidy and short-term grain-purchase credit** now, and apply once rules are published. Owner: finance with procurement. Driver: A0366 (government plans a new subsidy tranche and possible higher borrower limits). Transmission: lower rail cost and cheaper working capital. The measures are announced, not yet in force.
6. **Ask wheat suppliers which deliveries rely on Azov-Black Sea terminals**, and get Volga-route alternative quotes. Owner: sourcing. Driver: A0756 (key grain terminals in the Azov-Black Sea basin reported as effectively halted). Transmission: supply and freight route risk for durum imports.
7. **Keep packaging-film contracts at three months or less.** Owner: packaging buyer. Driver: A0727 (rising polymer capacity, mainly in Asia, may lower prices). Transmission: avoids locking in above-market prices.

## 2. Monitor

- **FX (A0311):** USD/RUB fell to about 82.5 and CNY/RUB to about 12.35 in a short move. Trigger: a sustained move of more than 5% over one month on the CNY rate. Then review hedging on China receivables. Do not lock rates on a six-day move.
- **Fuel exchange access rules (A1547):** still a government proposal. Trigger: final criteria published that affect our diesel supplier or delivery prices.
- **Azov-Black Sea port support (A0756):** trigger: support approved, or a terminal reopening announced, which would ease alternative-route pressure.
- **Wheat price (A0127):** trigger: cumulative rise above 3% over four weeks, which would justify raising the forward cover from one-third toward half.
- **Kazakhstan QazETA entry rules (A1408):** trigger: our drivers or staff need the permit for crossings.
