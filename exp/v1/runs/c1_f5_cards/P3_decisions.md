# GC memo: marketplace platform rules, seller terms and channel risk (week of 2026-10-21)

Basis: the 12 items as supplied. Several items are headlines only, so the analysis below does not rely on details beyond them.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Audit our own Ozon promotion participation within 14 days (owner: Commercial Legal + Ozon category leads).** Check every active promo for quantity caps and mid-promo removal. Where controls are lost, send written complaints to Ozon and document the losses for the seller union's FAS complaint. Driving items: A0443. Path: lost discount control leads to margin erosion and unsold stock at promo prices.
2. **Review our seller fee and agency contracts against the platform-economy law by 30 Nov (owner: Head of Contracts).** Confirm that every fee we charge sellers and every agency fee we pay to logistics or pickup partners has a defined service basis and is disclosed. Driving items: A0768, A0293. Path: a challenge to our fee model is direct legal and revenue exposure, since we run a marketplace.
3. **Run a self-audit of our own interface practices before a consumer complaint arrives, within 30 days (owner: Compliance).** Cover ranking, countdown timers, "last items" labels and default opt-ins at checkout and in promotions. Driving item: A1035 (headline only). Path: consumer-protection enforcement against the marketplace as operator.
4. **Protect own-brand supply on Wildberries (owner: Supply Chain + Legal, by 15 Nov).** Review WB contracts for force majeure, delivery-SLA penalties and stock-holding obligations. Hold at least 3 weeks of cover for the Ozon channel. Driving items: A0853, A0586. Path: WB logistics disruption leads to lost sales and contractual penalties.
5. **Do not co-sign the seller union's amendment request yet (owner: GC).** Prepare our own position paper on platform-seller balance for the Duma economic policy committee, so we can respond as operator when the bill is tabled. Driving item: A0768. Path: as operator, supporting seller-side fee limits cuts against our revenue.

## 2. Monitor (with trigger)

- **Platform-economy bill (A0768):** trigger is tabling or a committee hearing with fee caps or new seller-rights rules. Then escalate to Decision 5.
- **Ozon FAS complaint (A0443):** trigger is an FAS case or an Ozon rule change on promos. Then escalate Decision 1 to a formal response.
- **Ozon PVZ fee from 1 Dec (A0293):** trigger is a logistics partner passing the fee through to us. Then renegotiate pass-through terms.
- **Duma bill on online calls to riots (A0592):** trigger is a second reading or any platform-liability clause. Then review moderation of reviews and chats.
- **State social-support certificates on WB (A0486):** trigger is expansion to more regions or to our categories. Then assess whether we need an equivalent acceptance integration.
