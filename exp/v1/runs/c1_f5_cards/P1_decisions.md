# CFO memo: news week of 20–22 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Buy yuan in tranches, front-loaded.** Buy about 35% of the next 60 days of yuan requirement now, spread over 5 trading days. Buy a further 15% if CNY/RUB is at or below 12.20; pause new buying above 12.60. Owner: Treasury. Items A1342, A1570. Path: a stronger ruble makes yuan cheaper in rubles. Caveat: A1570 ties part of the rally to the October tax period, so do not assume it lasts past mid-November.
2. **Shift marketplace stock toward Ozon.** Move 10–15% of replenishment from Wildberries to Ozon for the next two months and rebalance ad spend to match. Owner: Commercial director. Items A0586, A0853. Path: Ozon's share of large-seller sales rose from 42% (January) to 58% (August), and drone damage to Wildberries adds fulfilment risk. The MPStats sample is top sellers, so check our own category data before scaling beyond 15%.
3. **No broad list-price increases in Q4.** Pass yuan savings only to top-selling SKUs where they defend share. Owner: Commercial director. Items A1545, A1098. Path: weekly CPI is 0.05% and year-to-date CPI is 6.15%, with household expectations down to 13.2%, so demand supports little price growth.
4. **No new fixed-rate term debt before the 23 Oct rate decision.** Keep ruble working-capital lines floating. Owner: CFO. Item A1081. Path: the regulator is expected to hold, and any cut is pushed to 2027, so locking long fixed rates now gains little.
5. **Open a second yuan settlement channel within 60 days.** Request yuan-settlement quotes from two banks with China-linked capacity. Owner: Treasury. Item A1592. Path: the Russia–China communiqué supports national-currency settlement and open banks, which reduces single-bank dependence. It states intent, not capacity, so verify with the banks.
6. **Audit payment-approval devices before 1 March 2027.** Remove any device with malware from the payment-approval chain. Owner: IT security. Item A1071. Path: from 1 March, banks must reject transfers from infected devices, which would block supplier payments.

## 2. Monitor

- **23 Oct CBR decision (A1081):** if the hold comes with a hawkish signal, fix about 30% of ruble lines for 12 months. A cut signal keeps us floating. The RSPP head's 2027 view is an opinion, not regulator guidance.
- **CNY/RUB below 12.00 or above 12.60:** resize the tranches in Decision 1 (A1342, A1570).
- **Possible Trump–Putin–Xi meeting around APEC in November (A0449, A1153):** treat as noise until signed. Revisit the payment channel only if banking or settlement sanctions are actually eased.
- **Weekly CPI above 0.20%:** reopen the pricing decision in Decision 3 (A1545).
- **Wildberries operations normalising:** revisit the stock split in Decision 2 (A0853).

## 3. Deliberately not acting on

- **A1661 (liability insurance tariffs for hazardous facilities, from 1 Nov):** applies to hazardous-facility owners. Check only whether our warehouses hold hazardous goods such as batteries.
- **A1342 dollar rate, and A0618 / A1395 (other currency moves):** we pay in yuan, so the dollar and these moves do not drive our hedges. A1395 is headline only, with no detail.
- **A0449 (MOEX index high):** equity moves have no direct link to our cash flows or debt.
- **A0825 (mortgage rates):** consumer lending, not our financing.
