# Memo: French protest wave, HR and Corporate Security (items to 21 Oct 2026)

**Assessment.** Protests since 17 Oct have become riots, arson and looting in Paris and other cities, with curfews in several regions (A0137, A0376, A1272, A0251). A new national action is set for Sat 24 Oct (A1935, A0616). Our Paris-region stores are directly exposed. The cards come from Russian-language media and I have not checked them against French sources. The hour and day thresholds below are my proposals, not figures from the items.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Sat 24 Oct store plan.** Close stores in any city under curfew or a prefectoral protest ban. Close Paris-region stores near announced demonstration routes at 17:00. Owner: Head of Store Safety with regional managers. Decide by Thu 23 Oct 12:00. Drivers: A0137, A1935, A1272. Path: riots and curfews lead to looting and lost trading hours.
2. **Shift and transport rule.** No one is required to work past a curfew. Staff who cannot reach work because of a curfew or closure are paid for scheduled hours for up to 3 days, and each store issues a safe-travel-home plan. Owner: HR labour relations. Decide by Thu 23 Oct. Drivers: A1272, A0616 (metro closure reported, verify with RATP). Path: curfew leads to absence and pay disputes.
3. **Under-18 staff.** Remove under-18 employees from evening shifts in affected cities, 23-25 Oct. Owner: store managers and HR. Drivers: A1745 (17 minors held overnight after protests). Path: crowds and detentions leads to minors' safety and night-work limit exposure.
4. **All-staff message.** Send by Thu 23 Oct 18:00, approved by legal and comms. Cover closures, who to call, no discipline for absence from unsafe travel, and no monitoring of lawful protest participation. Owner: crisis communication lead. Drivers: A1935, A0137.
5. **Store hardening in hotspot cities.** Reinforce shutters, move high-value displays away from windows, check CCTV, and confirm riot and civil-commotion cover with the insurer. Complete by Fri 23 Oct. Owner: Corporate Security with facilities. Drivers: A0551 (shops burned and looted), A0050 (kiosks burned). Path: looting leads to stock, property and closure losses.
6. **Travel advisory.** Postpone non-essential staff travel to the Paris region for 24-26 Oct. Owner: HR travel desk. Drivers: A1935, A0137.
7. **Daily risk review.** 08:00 check on 23-27 Oct with store security, comms and HR. Owner: Head of HR and Corporate Security.

## 2. Monitor

- **Turnout and bans on Sat 24 Oct (A1935, A1976).** Trigger: a ban in any city where we have stores. Then close those stores under item 1.
- **State of emergency (A0376).** Trigger: a formal declaration. Then apply emergency store-hour rules nationwide and re-run item 1.
- **Spread to Belgium, Italy and Spain (A0030, A0085, A0551).** Trigger: any protest wave reaching a city where we operate.
- **Curfew extension in Paris (A1272).** Trigger: extended past 26 Oct. Then extend item 2 pay cover.
