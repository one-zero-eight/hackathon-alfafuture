# P1 (CFO, Moscow consumer-electronics/appliance importer) - Whole-week decision memo

## 1. Key issues

**1. Ruble rally: yuan at multi-week lows, the buy-window question (A0311, A0323, A0606, A0618, A1342, A1570, A1327, A1395, A1409, A0131, A0453, A0649)**
- Path: the CBR yuan rate fell from 12.39 (Oct 20) to 12.37 (Oct 21) to 12.28 (Oct 22). MOEX yuan traded about 12.22-12.23 on Oct 21 and hit a monthly low of 12.35 on Oct 19. USD/RUB fell from about 85.8 to 81.97 in a week (lowest since Aug 7). CNY turnover was high (12.1 bn CNY, +21% vs prior-week average).
- Drivers named in the items: peace-talk optimism (A1409, A1342), the "diesel deal", Brent above $111 (A0453; then about $108 in A0649), exporters selling FX ahead of the quarterly tax date (A1570, A1327), and a current account surplus of $35 bn (A0131). Several of these are sentiment-driven and reversible.
- Why now: our yuan cost of goods is cheapest in about two months. The rally is partly tied to headline risk (talks, oil, Saudi attacks) and an end-of-month tax-date flow that fades. Experts disagree on whether it is permanent (A0618).

**2. Key rate stays at 14%, no cuts before 2027, loan costs stay high (A1081, A0700, A0089/A0825, A1588, A1026, A0250, A1060, A1098, A1545, A0167, A0492, A1815)**
- Path: the CBR meets Oct 23 and is expected to hold at 14% with a hawkish tone (Shohin). The last cut was 25 bp; the rate has been held since Sept 11.
- Supporting signals: OFZ 2038 cut-off yield is 16.74%. OFZ demand is concentrated in medium maturities as investors avoid long bonds (A1588). Max deposit rate is 13.06%. Population 1-year inflation expectations are 13.2%, and analysts raised their 2026-28 inflation forecasts to 7.1% / 5.0% / 4.2% (A1098). Weekly CPI is 0.05% (A1545).
- Fiscal tone is loose to neutral: a 2027 deficit of 2.2% of GDP and large spending on the ruling party's programme (A1815). The Finance Ministry gains weight with Siluanov as deputy PM (A0204, A0226, A1705).
- Why now: our ruble working-capital loans stay expensive and the 2027 budget should not assume relief. Floating-rate corporate paper is priced at key rate plus a spread (MTS, A0250: not above 180 bp). The corporate bond share is a record 31% (A0700), so bonds are a live alternative to bank loans. Banks are not being burdened with extra taxes for now (A1033).

**3. Sanctions and secondary-tariff risk is paused, not removed (A0037, A0083, A0115, A1999, A1349, A0183, A0448, A1170, A0765, A1356, A1564/A1628, A1848, A1284, A0777, A0390)**
- Path: Trump froze the "hellish sanctions" law, which would have allowed up to doubled or 100% tariffs on buyers of Russian energy, mainly China. He said he will not tighten sanctions while a deal is close. Trilateral talks are expected in the first half of November (Abu Dhabi), and APEC in Shenzhen is on Nov 18-19 with a possible Trump-Putin-Xi format.
- Limits: the freeze is reversible at will (A0115: Trump can sign "later or at any convenient time"; A0083 notes a 60-day horizon). The EU is going the other way, with a 22nd package targeting banks, the shadow fleet and energy (A0765, A1170). The OFAC Lukoil licence was extended only to Nov 21 (A1564, A1628).
- Transmission: secondary tariffs or tightening on China, or bank-level restrictions, would hit yuan payment channels and the cost of Chinese goods. Improved sentiment is what is strengthening the ruble, so a breakdown in talks around Nov 10-19 would reverse the FX move. Both risks cluster on the same dates.

**4. Yuan payment channel is politically backed, with residual friction (A0800, A1592, A0455, A0584, A1496, A0682, A0823, A0242, A0535, A0662, A1263)**
- Path: the RF-CN communique commits to keeping each side's banks operating and national-currency settlement near 100% (A0800, A1592). The SCO session still flags cross-border payment and customs frictions (A0584).
- Other friction items: the court dispute over customs revaluation of Chinese goods (A1263: customs used a database price three times the contract price), the crypto exchange Mexc exiting Russia (A0662, a closing grey route), and the Amsterdam court ruling that a bank cannot drop a Gazprom unit's account (A0535, only context).
- Why it matters: political support lowers the near-term risk of the main channel but does not remove bank-level and compliance friction. Diversifying banks and keeping documentation clean is cheap insurance.

**5. Landed-cost and customs pressure on imported electronics (A1120, A1263, A0967, A0474, A0205, A0865, A1556, A0242)**
- Path: the Industry Minister proposes raising import duties on any localised goods up to WTO-bound rates and expanding "second and third is excess" in procurement (A1120). Precedent items show customs challenging low declared values (A1263).
- Supply side: Samsung and SK Hynix are upgrading their Chinese memory plants but depend on annual US licences (A0967). China's rare-earth export controls are tightening (A0474; yttrium is critical for chips). China's exports grew 36.3% in September, with a record trade surplus (A0205), so Chinese suppliers have strong volume and pricing power.
- Logistics: the southern ports (Azov-Black Sea) are effectively down, with 95-100% drops in September throughput, and cargo is shifting to the Northwest and overland routes (A0865, A1556). Border-crossing upgrades are on the agenda (A0892). Longer or costlier routes lift landed cost and lead times.
- Why it matters: the strong ruble offsets some of this, but duty or customs-value moves and lead-time extension are not hedged by FX.

**6. Weak consumer value, falling average prices, shift in sales channels (A0128, A1098, A1345, A0409, A0586, A0853, A0443, A0768, A0293, A0594, A1975, A1632)**
- Path: Jan-Aug large appliance volume is +7.4% but value is -0.6%. Small appliances are +17.6% in volume and +13% in value (A0128), so average prices are falling, especially online. Consumer retail signals are soft (A1345, A0409). A premium niche is strong: iPhone Duo prices run from 299,990 rubles (A1975), with limited supply at launch (A1632).
- Channel: Ozon now holds about 58-60% of big-seller marketplace sales versus Wildberries, partly due to drone strikes on WB logistics (A0586, A0853). Marketplace terms are shifting against sellers: Ozon's promo conditions (A0443), platform-law fee transparency (A0768), and the PVZ agent fee from Dec 1 (A0293). VTB-WB card discount of up to 6% is a promo-competition signal (A0594).
- Why it matters: a stronger ruble lowers our ruble cost per yuan, but market price per unit is falling and our margin is squeezed from both ends. Pricing guidance must decide how much cost relief to pass on versus keep as margin.

**7. Drone and inventory risk on the retail and warehouse side (A0855, A0865, A0025, A0853)**
- Path: Krasnoe&Beloe now takes only 25-30% of earlier delivery volume per drop to limit stock at risk (A0855). WB warehouses are hit more than Ozon's. Drone attacks on Yandex data centres disrupted courts (A0025, minor).
- Why it matters: carrying less per node means more frequent, smaller deliveries (working-capital and logistics cost effects) and a possible need to spread stock over multiple warehouses and channels. It also shifts receivables and stock risk with marketplaces.

## 2. Decisions

1. **Tranche the yuan purchases now, do not wait for a lower print (A0311, A0323, A1342, A1570, A1327, A0453, A1409).** Buy a first tranche (suggest about 30-40% of the next 6-8 weeks of supplier payments) at the current 12.2-12.3 area. Add tranches on dips and on the days after the Oct 23 CBR meeting, and stop when the quarterly tax-date flow fades. Combines FX, oil and sanctions items; the aim is to lock the best level in about two months without betting on further gains.
2. **Hedge the rest with forwards or a rolling yuan position through the November risk window (A0037, A0083, A0115, A1848, A1284, A0390).** Cover the remaining exposure for Nov 10-20 (trilateral talks, APEC Nov 18-19, Lukoil licence expiry Nov 21, sanctions freeze reversibility). Size and instrument to be set by treasury; the aim is to protect against a reversal if talks fail. Requires reading the sanctions items together with the FX items.
3. **Keep ruble debt flexible, do not lock in long fixed rates (A1081, A0089/A0825, A1588, A1026, A0700, A0250).** Assume 14% key rate through 2026 and no cuts until 2027. Prefer short or floating-rate structures and negotiate spreads for the next renewals. Open a bond-market option study (a floater benchmarked on the MTS 180 bp spread and the record corporate-bond share) for part of the working capital. Do not refinance long at 16-17% yields.
4. **Raise cash buffer and liquidity lines before Nov (A1060, A0535, A1592, A0662, A0865).** Hold buffer equal to about 2 months of yuan payments plus a committed credit line, covering payment-channel disruption and port or route delays. Deposits at about 13% make holding the buffer relatively cheap.
5. **Split payment channels across at least 2-3 banks and document each flow (A1592, A0800, A0584, A0765, A0242, A1263, A0773).** Confirm each bank's yuan capacity, keep contract/customs value evidence (A1263 precedent), and check prepayment controls and counterparty validity (A0773, A1754 paper-VAT scrutiny). The EU 22nd package makes bank exposure a live risk.
6. **Pricing guidance: pass on only part of the ruble gain (A0128, A0586, A1098, A1545, A1120).** Tell the commercial team to hold list prices on appliances where value is falling, use the cost relief for selective promotion on high-volume SKUs, and keep a margin reserve in case duties (A1120) or the ruble reverse. Revisit after the Oct 23 meeting and mid-November.
7. **Rebalance channel economics and budget assumptions (A0586, A0853, A0443, A0768, A0293, A0594).** Shift part of the marketplace budget toward Ozon, but model the Ozon fee/promo changes (forced promotion participation, PVZ fee from Dec 1) before committing. Budget: FX base around 82-84 USD/RUB with downside sensitivity to 87, key rate 14% through 2026, and 2027 inflation under the 4% budget assumption treated as optimistic (A0167 versus A1098 forecasts).
8. **Contingency for logistics and customs (A0865, A1556, A1120, A0892, A0855).** Ask logistics to quote Northwest and overland routes and to book safety stock lead time of one extra cycle for Q4/New Year; plan smaller, more frequent deliveries to warehouses and avoid single-node stock concentration. Needs the logistics items and the working-capital items together.

## 4. Traps

- **Peace-talk headlines as proof of a permanent strong ruble (A1409, A1999, A1682, A0449, A1763, A0891):** the same news moved MOEX down 1% on Oct 21 (A1682) after +10% in the month; optimism is already priced and can reverse.
- **"Almost 100% national-currency settlement" (A0800, A0455, A0823):** political reassurance, not a guarantee of bank-level channel quality; A0584 itself flags payment frictions.
- **Oil spike as a reason to wait for an even stronger ruble (A0453, A0649):** oil dropped about 1.4% the same day and Saudi attacks are a risk-off driver, so it can swing both ways.
- **Hype on iPhone Duo (A1975, A1632):** premium foldable at 300-490 thousand rubles; limited relevance to a mid-size appliance importer's volume and no signal about our categories.
- **Low-relevance finance items:** Cifra Broker dollar bond at 9.5% (A0242, A0407), a small FX bond for a broker, not an option for us; black lenders (A0166); bank foreign investment rules (A0559); non-public ratings (A0554); candy excise denial (A0340); Mexc exit (A0662) except as a general channel sign.
- **Political or personnel items:** Siluanov promotion (A0204, A0226, A0176, A1705); Medvedev sanctions comments (A0448, A0481); prisoner talks (A0640); frozen assets in Euroclear (A1511, A1678); US debt (A0759); French yields (A1337) - background only.
- **Plastics and pesticide cases (A0727, A1263):** A1263 is a useful precedent on customs value, but the pesticide sector itself is not ours; A0727 concerns exporters of plastic, not our imports.
- **Ozon PVZ support and fee items (A0597, A0293):** affect PVZ owners more than us; only the seller promo and fee terms matter directly.
