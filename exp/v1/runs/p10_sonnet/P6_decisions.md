# HR and Security Memo: France unrest, "Act 2" on Sat 24 Oct

## 1. Decisions now
1. **Conditional closures for Sat 24 Oct (owner: Security Director with regional managers; decide Thu 22 Oct).** Close or shorten hours (close 14:00) only for stores within the demonstration routes and curfew zones in Paris, Lille, Marseille, Bordeaux, Toulouse, Lyon and Limoges. Stores outside those zones stay open. Items: A1935, A0616, A0050, A0225, A1873, A1976. Path: the unrest is concentrated in city centres, so the risk is looting and arson in those areas. Check: only the affected stores, not all 140.
2. **Pre-emptive protection (owner: Security, by Fri 23 Oct).** Board up windows and move high-value stock to the back for stores in those zones. Items: A0225, A0050, A0551. Path: the October 17 looting and arson hit shops and town halls. Check: scope is the same affected-store list, and the measures are reversible. This applies pattern (d), riots plus prior vandalism.
3. **Staff schedules and transport (owner: HR Operations, by Fri 23 Oct).** Allow flexible start times and early finish, and keep staff out of the building at night. Book taxis or vehicles for closing teams in curfew zones. Items: A0616, A1504, A0475, A1873. Path: the Paris curfew has been extended and the metro was reported closed, so staff cannot get home. Check: the curfew is temporary and renewed night by night, so I will check each prefecture decision rather than assume a duration. This applies pattern (a), transport disruption plus demonstration.
4. **Curfew-aware opening hours (owner: Regional managers, daily).** Close affected stores early enough for staff to leave before curfew. Items: A1504, A0251, A0475, A1873. Path: curfews are spreading to other cities. Check: apply only in cities with a curfew, and review nightly.
5. **Staff protection and incident reporting (owner: Security, by Fri 23 Oct).** Brief managers in affected stores, with an evacuation and lock-down procedure and a single incident-reporting line. Items: A0050, A0605, A0593. Path: the government is promising a tougher response, which means clashes near stores. Check: safety comes first over sales, and the briefing is proportionate.
6. **Employee communication (owner: Internal Comms, SMS and email Thu 22 and Fri 23 Oct, then every few hours on Sat).** Give clear instructions on which stores are open, what pay applies, and who to call. Items: A1935, A0616, A0587. Path: staff need instructions before the weekend. Check: the message is limited to the affected stores.
7. **Pay and labour relations for closures (owner: HR, with CSE by Fri 23 Oct).** Staff in closed stores are paid or rescheduled. Inform the CSE and open dialogue with unions. Items: A0030, A1240, A0551. Path: the demonstrations are about living costs, so union tension is likely. Check: the measure is temporary, covers this weekend only, and the CSE is consulted before any collective change.
8. **Staff travel (owner: HR, immediately).** Defer non-essential staff travel to Italy, Belgium and Spain until protests ease. Items: A0085, A0030, A0551, A0139. Path: unrest has spread to those countries. Check: this is travel only, and I will not recall staff without a government advisory.
9. **Prepare for a state of emergency (owner: Crisis Cell, by Sat 24 Oct).** Draft a plan for emergency measures. Items: A0376, A0605. Path: the Prime Minister did not rule one out. Check: this is a draft only, and nothing changes until the declaration.

## 2. Monitor
- Declaration of a state of emergency (A0376), which would trigger the draft plan in item 9.
- Whether curfews spread to more cities (A0475, A0251). Trigger: a curfew in a city where we have stores means an immediate early-closing review.
- The Thursday student protest ban in Paris (A1976). Trigger: protests despite the ban means closing the nearby stores Thursday.
- Shortages from truck and farmer protests (A0578, A1369, A1807). Trigger: delays to our deliveries mean supply planning.
- Military support for the police (A0593).

## 3. Deliberately not acting on

- Russian officials' remarks (A0246, A0248, A1712): not operational.
- US and Trump (A0787), market commentary (A0612) and the protest ranking (A1843): not relevant.
- The claim that Parisians are attacking migrant neighbourhoods (A1575): unverified, and it needs no separate action.
