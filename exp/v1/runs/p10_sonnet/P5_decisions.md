# COO Memo: Week of 19-21 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fuel surcharge up, carrier talks (this week, owner: Commercial + Procurement).** Raise the fuel surcharge on all truck and rail-linked contracts, with a monthly indexation clause tied to diesel. Open renegotiation with the top 5 carriers and ask for 60-90 day price caps. Items: A1072 (EU fuel +31.4% y/y, diesel +5.9% m/m), Brent above $111 (A0453), A0123 (fuel crisis). Path: carrier costs rise, then our margin is squeezed. Check: direction is rising, so surcharge and renegotiate, and do not lock long fixed fuel contracts. Cross-signal (d): reprice all contracts, as the tariff and wagon pressure in A0123 and A0126 come on top of fuel.

2. **Black Sea closure leads to Caspian and Middle Corridor capacity (book within 7 days; owner: Route Planning).** Raise Middle Corridor and Caspian bookings by about 20% for Nov-Dec, with a 3-week buffer on lead times (normal lead time 2-4 weeks). Keep the northern route as the base case. Items: A0123 (Black Sea closed, Turkey-Iran transit cost doubled), A0900, A0865, A0408. Path: the southern Russian ports are disrupted, so more cargo seeks alternative corridors and capacity gets scarce. Check: the Black Sea attacks look persistent (NATO is now involved), not temporary, but news on Georgian and Azerbaijani infrastructure is absent, so add buffer and do not commit fully.

3. **Reprice for scarce capacity (this week, owner: Pricing).** Add a 10-15% scarcity premium on China-Kazakhstan-Russia rail and truck quotes and a congestion surcharge on Georgia-bound trucks. Items: A0123 (Chinese delivery pricier because of the Kazakh border), A0126 (Mintrans reallocating wagons to Baltic and Oktyabrskaya line, coal pushing out other cargo), A1450 (133 trucks queued at Verkhniy Lars, +16 in one hour). Path: wagon and border constraints reduce capacity. Check: raise prices when capacity is scarce (rule 11). Cross-signal (c): wagon shortage plus demand surge (trucking demand +20%), so lock wagon fleet contracts for Nov-Dec now and move urgent cargo to trucks.

4. **Alternative crossings, pre-booking (2-3 weeks ahead, owner: Route Planning).** Reroute about 15% of volume currently through the most congested Kazakh crossings to alternatives, and pre-book wagons for 3 weeks out. Items: A0123, A1450. Path: border crossing difficulty raises delivery cost and time. Check: the queue evidence is from one day, so treat it as possibly temporary and keep the shift reversible and partial.

5. **Dual-use and drone screening, effective now (owner: Compliance).** Place a hold on HS codes covering drones, autonomous-flight, radio-control and anti-jamming satellite navigation items shipped from China, and require end-use declarations. China's new export controls start 18 Nov, so require an export licence copy for any such cargo loading after that date. Items: A0285, A1728. Path: Chinese licensing creates documentation and legal risk for cargo transiting Kazakhstan to Russia. Check: the strictest of the Chinese, EU, US and Kazakh/EAEU regimes governs; the Chinese one is newly binding.

6. **Iran-linked screening and payment channels (this week, owner: Compliance + Finance).** Screen all clients, consignees and legal and agent counterparties against the new OFAC listing (A1728, A1762), including any Iran-linked routing. Require prepayment from Russia-bound clients with bank exposure and cut any Russia-bound credit lines by half until payment channels are verified. Path: US secondary sanctions risk reaches us through banks and counterparties. Check: US secondary sanctions apply to dollar flows even though we are in Kazakhstan, so the strictest regime governs. Cross-signal (a): sanctions tightening plus pressure on banks, so drop risky clients.

7. **Client notice on Kazakh entry rules and Estonia hours (within 5 days, owner: Customer Service).** Send an advisory to clients on QazETA electronic entry permits for driver crews (A1408) and on the Estonian checkpoint hours change from 25 Oct (A0958, A1559). Update documentation checklists. Path: customs and entry rule changes delay crews and cargo. Check: the rule is binding for Russia-bound customers, so delay shipments until compliant.

## 2. Monitor

- **Black Sea shipping (A0900, A0239, A0644):** trigger to act again is a NATO naval escort announcement or ports reopening, which would cut Middle Corridor demand and let us release extra bookings.
- **Brent (A0453):** if it moves above $120 or falls below $95, reopen the fuel contract position.
- **Kazakh border queue (A1450):** if the queue exceeds 200 trucks for 5 days, treat it as structural and shift 30% of volume.
- **Tenge:** no item this week, but watch USD pricing if the tenge weakens by 3% (rule 9).
