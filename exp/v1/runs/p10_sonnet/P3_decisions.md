# GC Memo, week of 2026-10-21

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Freeze contentious platform-rule changes (promo terms, commissions, ranking, fees) for 30 days; open a documentation file.** Owner: GC with Commercial. Items: A0443, A0768, A0586, A0293. Path: sellers are complaining to FAS about Ozon's promo terms, and the Union of Marketplace Sellers (СПМ) has written to FAS about platform imbalance. Our own-brand sales and our marketplace operations are both exposed to the same theory of harm. Rules 5 and 8; pattern (d). Direction check: the complaints are aimed at Ozon, not us, but a FAS probe usually widens to the whole sector. Exposure is real, since we run a platform and also sell our own brands. Only changes already under way that cut seller risk are exempt.

2. **Within 10 days, audit our seller promo and discount terms against the A0443 complaints.** Owner: Legal and Seller Ops. Check whether sellers can cap discounted volume and withdraw items from sales. Where they cannot, draft an amendment giving them those rights, ready to issue but held until the freeze is lifted. Items: A0443. Rule 8. Direction check: this reduces risk and costs little, and sellers can reverse it.

3. **File our written position on the platform-economy law with the Duma economic policy committee and through АКИТ, within 2 weeks.** Owner: GC and Government Relations. Items: A0768, A0421. Path: СПМ is lobbying to amend the law, so the window for shaping the text is open now. Rule 1 and pattern (b). Direction check: СПМ's text is a request for amendment, not an adopted law. Our contracts stay unchanged until adoption and the effective date are known. This is a comment-stage action.

4. **Join the АКИТ initiative for an emergency operating regime during state-system (ГИС) outages. Draft a contingency clause for seller contracts in parallel.** Owner: GC and Operations. Items: A0421, A1801. Path: new marking categories, enlarging the Честный знак system, depend on state systems. If those systems fail, shipments halt and we face seller claims. Rule 6. Direction check: АКИТ's proposal is only a letter to the prime minister. The clause is temporary and costs little.

5. **Run a unified seller rollout for the 1 Nov Честный знак expansion, with a seller notice by 27 Oct.** Owner: Compliance. Items: A1801, A0762. Path: the new marked categories apply to sellers on both our platform and our own-brand listings from 1 Nov. Marking and anti-fraud listing checks can go out in one notice. Rule 6 and pattern (c). Direction check: this measure is permanent and takes effect in 10 days. The exact product list is not in the card, so Compliance must confirm it against the primary legal text before the notice goes out.

6. **Tighten the notice-and-takedown path for fake brand storefronts, with a 48-hour SLA for brand-holder reports.** Owner: Trust and Safety. Items: A0762. Path: fraudsters impersonating Russian brands raise counterfeit-liability risk and customer-fraud claims. Rule 7. Direction check: the F6 data covers web resources generally, not our platform. The change is a process adjustment and sellers can reverse it.

7. **Within 2 weeks, review the storefront for countdown timers and scarcity counters, and set a rule that they must reflect real stock.** Owner: Legal and Product. Items: A1035. Path: the consumer union calls these practices manipulative, and regulators may follow. Rules 4 and 6. Direction check: no new law is cited. This is advance hygiene. Treat it as an ad and consumer-protection review, not a response to an enacted rule.

8. **Patch review for the PHP vulnerability: Security to confirm within 72 hours which of our sites and seller portals run PHP, and to patch. Pre-draft the breach-notification procedure for Roskomnadzor.** Owner: CISO and GC. Items: A1730. Path: a compromised site could expose customer personal data, which carries a 24-72 hour reporting duty under Russian rules. Rule 3. Direction check: the Russian regime governs, not GDPR. The threat is described only as potential, so this is verification, not a confirmed incident.

## 2. Monitor

- FAS opening a case or sending an inquiry about Ozon promos, or any other platform. Trigger: a letter to us or a published case. Then we move from documentation to a defence (A0443, A0768).
- The text and reading dates of any platform-economy law amendments. Trigger: a bill is introduced, at which point we move to comments (A0768).
- Ozon's PVZ fee from December. Trigger: any pickup-point operator complaint that reaches us. Then we review our own pickup-point contract terms (A0293).
- Any Russian incident linked to the PHP flaw. Trigger: a confirmed attack on a Russian retailer (A1730).
