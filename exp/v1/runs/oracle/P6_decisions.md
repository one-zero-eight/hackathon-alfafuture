# Decision memo: French protest wave (week of 17 Oct) and planned 24 Oct "Act 2"

From: Head of HR and Corporate Security
Scope: 140 stores, ~4,500 employees, Paris region and large French cities

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Map every store against curfew zones.** Paris curfew is in force in parts of the city, including Place de la Republique, and has been extended for a third night (23:00 to 06:00) [A1873]. Prefects and mayors are setting further local curfews [A0475, A0251]. Action: list stores inside any published curfew zone; close them before curfew start; reopen only after the prefecture lifts the zone. Drivers: A1873, A0475, A0251.
2. **Early closure of exposed stores on Sat 24 Oct.** Authorities expect a large "Act 2" wave with looting and clashes [A1935, A0605]. Looting and arson hit shops and public buildings last weekend [A0225]. Action: close or shorten hours for stores on likely protest routes and in the Paris core; decide by 23 Oct after the prefecture publishes its plan.
3. **Do not rely on army cover.** Operation Sentinelle soldiers will guard fixed sites to free police [A0593]. Action: ask the prefecture in writing whether our stores are on the protected list; fund our own shutter, guarding and alarm checks where not confirmed.
4. **Flexible scheduling for staff.** Metro closures and burnt vehicles disrupt commuting [A0616]. Action: allow early departure and shift swaps for staff in affected areas, with no penalty for lateness caused by closures. Do not impose unpaid absence without legal review. Labor and pay position: to be set with labor relations and legal before 24 Oct.
5. **Travel advisory.** Action: advise staff and business travellers to Paris to expect transport disruption on 24 Oct and during curfew nights [A0616, A1873].
6. **Staff communication.** Action: send a short notice to all staff with curfew and closure times, safety rules (no confrontation, leave when told), and a hotline. Do not use crowd-control language.

## 2. Monitor

- Whether a state of emergency is declared; this could change opening-hour rules and staff powers [A0376].
- Prefecture decisions on banning unauthorised demonstrations and on police tactics before 24 Oct [A1935].
- Stolen goods resold on platforms; government wants them removed and sellers prosecuted [A0367]. Action: loss prevention checks on resale sites for our stock and SKUs.
- Curfew map updates from A0251 and A0475 for new cities.

## 3. Ignored as not relevant to our operations

- A0605 is covered by decision 2 and monitoring; no separate action.
- A0225 is political context only; no action beyond decision 2.

Note: no item gives store-level facts, so decisions rest on the stated curfew and deployment plans, not on confirmed risk at our sites.
