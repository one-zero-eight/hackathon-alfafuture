# Decision memo: Procurement & Supply Chain (Volga pasta and bakery producer)
Week of 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-21

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Cover part of Q4/Q1 durum wheat and flour needs with forward purchases.** Drivers: A0127 (domestic wheat up 0.4-1.2% weekly after three months of decline, with slow growth expected to continue as farmers resist low-price sales). Hedge a portion, not the full need, since the rise is modest.
2. **Lock rail capacity and carriers for export shipments to Kazakhstan and China ahead of demand.** Drivers: A0123 (freight demand up 20% in Q3; China-bound cargo costs rising because of border-crossing difficulties with Kazakhstan) and A0126 (rail capacity is being reallocated toward the Oktyabrskaya line in October). Book early and add a rail-based backup.
3. **Re-check truck consignments for QR-code compliance before dispatch on the Kazakhstan route.** Driver: A0826 (713 trucks turned back at the Tyumen/Kurgan border for QR-code non-compliance). Shift marginal volumes to rail where feasible.
4. **Apply for the rail transport subsidy and short-term grain purchase loans where we qualify.** Driver: A0366 (new rail transport subsidy tranche; possible higher per-borrower limits on grain-purchase credit). Lowers rail export and raw-material costs.
5. **Keep packaging film contracts short (3-6 months) rather than long-term fixed.** Driver: A0727 (rising Asian polymer capacity may soften prices and shrink the export niche for Russian polymer producers). Avoids locking in current prices.
6. **Denominate export contracts in CNY or USD, not RUB, for now.** Driver: A0311 (ruble strengthening: USD near 82.5 RUB, CNY near 12.35 RUB). Strengthening RUB reduces ruble value of foreign-currency sales.

## 2. Monitor

- **A1547** (exchange trading of petroleum products may be restricted; diesel price stability measures): diesel is our trucking cost driver. Watch for surcharge changes from carriers.
- **A0756** (Azov-Black Sea grain terminals largely suspended; support under review): could affect southern grain and durum sourcing and port-based logistics. Confirm our supply origins.
- **A0366** and **A0756**: whether the subsidies are approved and their eligibility criteria.
- **A0311**: further ruble moves before finalizing currency terms.

## 3. Ignored (not relevant to our decisions)

- **A1408**: entry rules for foreign visitors to Kazakhstan (QazETA). No direct effect on cargo. Revisit only if our drivers are foreign nationals.
