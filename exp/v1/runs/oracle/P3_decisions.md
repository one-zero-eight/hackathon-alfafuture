# Decision memo: news monitoring, week of 2026-10-16 to 21

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Run a PHP security audit of our storefront and marketplace stack, and patch.** Driven by A1730 (critical PHP flaws found with AI tooling, tens of thousands of Runet sites at risk). Transmission: an exploit could expose customer personal data, which triggers breach-notification duties. Scope depends on whether our stack runs PHP; this is an assumption to confirm.
2. **Audit urgency and scarcity widgets on our marketplace (countdown timers, "only N left") and remove any that are not accurate.** Driven by A1035 (consumer union on manipulative practices). Transmission: consumer-protection complaints and fines.
3. **Review advertising claims in our campaigns and on our brand listings.** Driven by A0182 (FAS found MegaFon's "5G mode" ad misleading). Transmission: FAS can find ads false and penalize us as advertiser and marketplace operator.
4. **Set up a legal review of our promotion participation terms and check our own marketplace promo rules.** Driven by A0443 (sellers complain to FAS about Ozon promo terms). Transmission: we sell our brands on Ozon and Wildberries and run promotions, so the same scrutiny could reach our terms.
5. **Reconcile our court-deadline calendar and set up backup filing routes.** Driven by A0025 (arbitration hearings postponed after attacks on Yandex data centers). Transmission: missed hearing dates and deadlines in our active cases.
6. **Build a position paper and engage with ФАС and the Duma through our trade associations.** Driven by A0768 (seller union seeks changes to the platform economy law). Transmission: possible limits on platform fees and marketplace obligations that affect our model. Decision authority: regulatory engagement.

## 2. Monitor

- **A0768**: whether the Duma or the administration takes up the platform-economy amendments.
- **A0443**: whether FAS opens a case on Ozon promotions.
- **A1801**: scope of the Chestny Znak expansion from 1 Nov 2026. Check our SKUs against the new categories. The item is truncated, so details are unknown.
- **A0421**: whether a government emergency-operation mode for GIS outages is adopted, which affects marking and reporting.
- **A0478**: whether the VMmanager breach extends to hosting providers we use. Check vendor exposure.
- **A0025**: whether the court service disruption continues.
