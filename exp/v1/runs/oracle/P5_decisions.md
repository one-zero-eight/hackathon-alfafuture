# Decision memo: COO, Almaty rail/truck forwarder (China-Kazakhstan-Russia, Middle Corridor)
Date: 2026-10-22. Basis: the 10 news items supplied only.

## 1. Decisions and actions now

1. **Enforce QR-code pre-check on all truck consignments into Russia via Tyumen/Kurgan.** Driven by A0826: Tyumen customs turned back 713 trucks from Kazakhstan in 9 months over QR-code non-compliance. Turned-back trucks mean lost fees and driver costs, so every consignment is verified before dispatch.
2. **Add a border-crossing surcharge on China-to-Russia-via-Kazakhstan services and hold extra transit buffer.** Driven by A0123: China-origin delivery costs are rising because of Kazakhstan-border difficulties. Pricing must reflect this, and quoted transit times must include slack.
3. **Index carrier fuel surcharges to diesel and review fuel clauses.** Driven by A0453 (Brent above $111) and A1547 (diesel price-stabilisation measures under discussion). Fuel is our largest variable cost, so surcharges must move with the index.
4. **Pre-screen drone-related and dual-use cargo before the 18 November Chinese export-control change.** Driven by A0285. Shipments with drones, autonomous-flight, radio-control or satellite-navigation components need a licence check.
5. **Screen clients and counterparties against the EU 22nd package scope.** Driven by A0765 (planned restrictions on shadow-fleet vessels and energy and banking figures). Our European importers need enhanced due diligence.

## 2. Monitor (item IDs)

- **A0865, A0126**: Southern Russian port losses shift cargo to land and northwest routes, and coal is being rerouted onto the October Railway. Watch rail slot availability and pricing on Russian routes; secure capacity early.
- **A0300**: Iranian call for Russian Caspian port protection, citing Ukrainian attacks. Assess security and insurance risk on Caspian legs of the Middle Corridor.
- **A0037**: US "hell sanctions" law temporarily frozen. Track whether secondary measures return, since they could hit buyers of Russian energy.
- **A0765**: Confirm final EU package content before adjusting client terms.
- **A1547**: Confirm any exchange-access limits affecting diesel supply.

## 3. Ignored as not relevant

- A0126 (coal volumes) is domestic Russian freight and has only indirect effect on our routes; it is listed under monitoring above, not acted on.

Note: all items are Russian-language summaries; no figures beyond the items have been assumed.
