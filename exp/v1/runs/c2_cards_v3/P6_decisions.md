# HR and Corporate Security memo: French unrest, week of 19-22 Oct 2026

Themes: (1) city riots and curfews; (2) Saturday 24 Oct "Act 2" mobilisation; (3) looting and property damage; (4) transport and staff movement; (5) court and detention protests; (6) possible state of emergency; (7) spread to Italy, Spain and Belgium; (8) cost-of-living grievances; (9) political commentary.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Close or shorten hours at stores near routes in riot-hit cities, Sat 24 Oct.** Scope: stores within about 1 km of any announced demonstration route or damaged site in Paris, Lille, Marseille, Bordeaux and Toulouse. Close at 14:00 or at the curfew start, whichever is earlier. Owner: Store Safety. Items: A1935, A0137, A1620. Path: the new "Act 2" wave repeats last weekend's looting and arson near retail. Check: only the affected subset closes, not all 140 stores; closing reduces risk and the sales loss is accepted.

2. **Board up and protect stock at exposed stores, by Fri 23 Oct 18:00.** Covers the Decision 1 zone plus Paris-region stores on demonstration routes. Board street-facing windows, move high-value apparel and home goods to back rooms, and add one guard per store on Saturday evening. Owner: Corporate Security. Items: A0137 (looting), A0050 (kiosks burned, city halls stormed). Path: looting follows crowds on riot days. Cross-signal (d): riots plus prior vandalism calls for pre-emptive closure and stock protection. Check: boarding is reversible and cheap.

3. **Cap store hours inside curfew zones.** Confirm each city's curfew start from prefect orders daily; affected stores close at least 30 minutes before it. Owner: Store Operations. Items: A1504 (Paris curfew, third night), A1272 (Paris curfew extended), A0475 (curfews spreading to other cities). Path: curfew is binding. Check: temporary, reviewed daily, no permanent change.

4. **Flexible shifts and staff transport, 23-26 Oct.** Offer flexible start and early finish. Arrange taxis or shuttles where transit is disrupted, after confirming the metro status with RATP. Keep full pay for hours we cancel. Owner: HR Scheduling. Items: A0616 (metro closure reported in one personal account only), A1935. Path: transit disruption cuts staff arrival. Check: lead time is 2 days, inside the 2-5 day window; duration is limited to the announced dates.

5. **Staff alert by SMS and email by 23 Oct 18:00, then every 4 hours on 24 Oct.** Content: store closure status, transport options, and a instruction to avoid demonstration areas. Accept shift-swap requests without penalty for 23-26 Oct. Owner: HR Communication. Items: A1935, A1745 (students and schoolchildren protesting). Path: young staff and students may join protests. Check: safety over sales; no monitoring of protest participation.

6. **Pre-draft a wider Paris closure for 25 Oct, ready to trigger.** Trigger: a ban on the 24 Oct protest, a state-of-emergency decree, or clashes near our stores. Owner: Corporate Security. Items: A0376 (state of emergency not ruled out), A1935 (ban under study), A1976 (Paris banned a student action on Thursday). Check: proportional; no pre-closure of all 140 stores.

## 2. Monitor

- **Sat 24 Oct turnout and any ban (A1935, A0605):** if the prefect bans the demonstration or clashes occur near our stores, extend Decision 1 closures to Sunday 25 Oct and activate Decision 6.
- **State-of-emergency decree (A0376):** reassess all hours against prefect orders.
- **Court and detention protests (A1745, A1620):** if protests move into Paris shopping streets, add guards to the Decision 1 zone.
- **Spread to Italy, Spain and Belgium (A0551, A0085, A0030):** if the French foreign ministry issues an advisory, or staff trips are booked there, restrict non-essential travel (Rule 5). The Russian ministry advice (A1017) is for Russian citizens and does not apply.
- **Union or NAO activity (A0376 cites cost of living and fuel prices):** a formal strike notice or NAO invitation triggers early dialogue and a costed modest-raise option (Rule 7). Nothing in the items yet.
