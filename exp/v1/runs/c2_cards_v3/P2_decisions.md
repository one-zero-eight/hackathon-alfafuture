# Procurement memo, week of 21 October 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Forward-buy durum and flour.** Cover 2 months of requirement now: 50% fixed-price, 50% index-linked. Qualify one additional mill this month. Hold buffer at 3-4 weeks. Owner: grain buyer. Driver: A0127 (wheat up 0.4-1.2% weekly after a three-month fall, trend expected to continue). Path: domestic grain cost rises. Check: rising prices favour buying early; capped at 2 months because the rise is modest and not supply-driven.

2. **Verify QR codes on Kazakhstan truck lots.** Before dispatch, check the QR code on every goods batch; hold non-compliant lots at origin. Owner: export compliance. Driver: A0826 (713 trucks turned back at Tyumen/Kurgan over QR non-compliance in nine months). Path: refused entry and return cost. Check: documentation first (rule 6); enforcement is ongoing, so treated as permanent.

3. **Keep packaging film on 1-2 month contracts.** Do not lock 3-6 months. Add a polymer-price review to renewals. Owner: packaging buyer. Driver: A0727 (Chinese polymer capacity growth). Path: polymer prices likely soften. Check: lock-in applies to spikes (rule 4); none seen, and capacity growth points down, so no lock at current levels.

4. **Defer imported packaging; re-price ruble export quotes.** No pre-buying of imported inputs; re-quote Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and China prices. Owner: finance and procurement. Drivers: A0618 (USD 82.89 versus about 87 in September, roughly 5% stronger), with A0574 and A1220 (diesel deal cited as the cause). Path: cheaper imports, thinner export margins. Check: rule 7 reversed; experts disagree whether the strength is temporary, so no cover beyond normal orders.

5. **Reserve rail slots for Kazakhstan and China.** Book 2-4 weeks ahead for the next three weeks of volume plus a 20% buffer; keep one truck carrier for urgent small lots. Owner: logistics. Drivers: A0865 (southern port cargo shifting to northwest and land crossings) and A0826 (border truck flow). Path: more volume competing for rail and land capacity. Check: rail lead time is 1-3 weeks, so booking comes first; congestion rises, so book early.

6. **Add a fuel-index surcharge clause at carrier renewal.** No mid-contract renegotiation. Owner: logistics. Drivers: A0574 (FAS says partial diesel export easing will not raise domestic prices), A1220. Path: fuel cost. Check: rule 8 triggers only on an actual fuel rise; none confirmed, so clause only.

## 2. Monitor

- **A0127 / grain export duty or quota:** any change triggers a forward review under rule 3 (duty rise: delay forwards; removal: buy forward).
- **A0618 ruble:** USD sustained below 80, or back above 87, triggers a review of import timing and export quotes.
- **A1220 / A0574 / A1182 diesel:** A1220 says Russia lacks fuel for export, which conflicts with FAS. If regional diesel prices rise or the price-cap agreements lapse, renegotiate freight (rule 8).
- **A0865 rail:** wagon lead times above 3 weeks switch urgent lots to trucks.
- **A0727 polymer:** contract polymer prices falling at renewal lowers the indexation base.
