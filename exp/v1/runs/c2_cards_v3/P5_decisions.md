# COO memo: news week of 18-21 Oct 2026

## 1. Decisions now

1. **QR-code gate on Russia-bound trucks.** Verify the QR code on every consignment batch before dispatch on Tyumen/Kurgan truck routes; hold any batch without it. Owner: Operations, effective immediately, plus a 1-2 day transit buffer. Items: A0826, A0922. Path: 713 trucks were turned back for QR non-compliance in nine months, so unverified batches get returned at the border. Direction check: enforcement of an existing rule, so permanent; build it into dispatch.

2. **QazETA check for crews.** Confirm whether any foreign-national drivers or staff on our routes need QazETA entry permits; brief crews before the next crossing. Owner: Operations/HR, within 1 week. Item: A1408. Path: new Kazakh entry rules for visa-free states, including Russia. Direction check: applies to people entering Kazakhstan, so it affects crews, not cargo.

3. **Screening refresh for EU 22nd package.** Add shadow-fleet, energy and banking-sector designees to client and cargo screening; hold new Russia-bound clients to prepayment. Owner: Compliance, this week. Item: A0765. Path: EU tightening plus bank exposure (pattern a). Direction check: the package is only proposed, but screening is cheap and reversible; apply the strictest regime (EU, US, EAEU).

4. **US secondary-sanctions screen.** Add the new OFAC Iran-linked designations to screening; verify USD payment channels for any Iran-linked counterparty. Owner: Compliance/Finance, within 1 week. Items: A1728, A1762. Path: US designations raise secondary risk on bank channels (rule 4). Direction check: exposure is only via counterparties, so screen, don't reprice.

5. **Drone and dual-use hold.** Screen existing bookings for drone-related HS codes and autonomy/radio-control technology by 31 Oct; from 18 Nov require a Chinese export licence for those shipments, and hold any that lack one. Owner: Compliance. Item: A0285. Path: China's new export controls (rule 8, delay until compliant). Direction check: rule is permanent once in force, and the hold is reversible.

6. **Rail and Caspian contingency.** If Russia-bound volumes start moving away from Azov-Black Sea ports, pre-book rail wagons and Caspian capacity 2-3 weeks ahead; add a surcharge if wagons are short. Owner: Commercial/Operations. Items: A1556, A0865, A0503. Path: port outages push cargo to northern and Caspian routes (pattern b). Direction check: outages are attack-driven and may be temporary, so book at the trigger, not in advance.

7. **Client notice.** Tell Russia-bound clients about the QR-code rule, QazETA, and possible port-driven rerouting, with alternative routes. Owner: Commercial, this week. Items: A0826, A1408, A1556. Path: rules 8 and 10. Direction check: communication only, no cost.
