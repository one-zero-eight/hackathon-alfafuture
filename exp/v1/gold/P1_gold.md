# P1 Gold Memo — CFO, Moscow consumer-electronics/appliance importer (week of 16–21 Oct 2026)

## 1. Key issues

**1. Sharp ruble rally: yuan at multi-week lows, a buying window** — A0311, A0323, A0606, A1342, A1570, A1327, A1395, A0618
- What happened: CNY/RUB fell from about 12.39 (official rate for 20 Oct) to 12.2255 on MOEX on 21 Oct. The official rate for 22 Oct is 12.28. USD fell below 82 for the first time since 7 Aug. The ruble rose four days in a row on heavy volume (yuan turnover +21% vs the prior week's average), even while the CBR was buying FX.
- Drivers named: Brent above $108–111, exporters selling FX before the October tax period, and peace-talk optimism.
- Transmission: yuan supplier payments get cheaper in rubles, so landed cost falls directly.
- Why now: the tax-period support is seasonal and fades after late October. Experts disagree whether the rally is structural or temporary (A0618).

**2. Geopolitics/sanctions de-escalation is the swing factor for the ruble** — A0037, A0115, A0083, A1999, A1409, A1848, A1284, A0883, A1763, A0518, A0891, A1356, A0777, A0390, A1682, A0449
- US: Trump froze the "hellish sanctions" (Graham) law, which threatened 100% tariffs on China as a buyer of Russian energy, and said he will not tighten sanctions. Trilateral talks with Ukraine are slated for November in Abu Dhabi, and a Trump–Putin–Xi meeting is possible at APEC Shenzhen on 18–19 Nov.
- Transmission (two channels):
  - Lower secondary-sanction risk for Chinese banks that handle our payments.
  - Ruble sentiment.
- Why now: the waiver can be revoked "at any time" (A0115, A0083). The MOEX index fell 1% after the Witkoff meeting, which shows the optimism is fragile (A1682). The rally is partly headline-driven and can reverse on a failed talks round.

**3. Russia–China payment channel looks stable, but frictions remain** — A0800, A1592, A0455, A0584, A1496, A0682, A0823, A0892
- Positives: national-currency share in settlements is near 100%. The joint communiqué commits to supporting each other's banks operating on both territories and to keeping national-currency settlement.
- Frictions: SCO discussion acknowledged cross-border payment and customs frictions persist (A0584).
- Counter-signal: the EU's 22nd package targets the banking sector (A0765), and Mexc's exit shows that alternative rails keep shrinking (A0662).
- Transmission: reliability and cost of yuan payments to suppliers.

**4. Rates stay high: CBR meets 23 Oct; hold with a hawkish tone is expected** — A1081, A1588, A1026, A1098, A1545, A0825, A1060, A0250, A0700
- Expectation: the key rate is 14%. Shokhin expects a hold, a hawkish tone, and no cuts before 2027.
- Market signals: OFZ demand favoured the medium tenor, and the long OFZ yield is 16.74%.
- Inflation:
  - Analysts raised their 2026–28 forecasts (7.1% / 5.0% / 4.2%).
  - Household expectations fell to 13.2%, but the 5-year horizon rose to 12.2%.
  - Weekly CPI slowed to 0.05%.
- Funding benchmarks: the CBR path implies an average key rate of 10.5–12.5% in 2027 (A0825). The MTS floater is priced at key rate + ≤180bp. Deposits pay about 13%. Bonds are a record 31% of corporate funding.
- Transmission: the cost of ruble working-capital loans and the choice between fixed and floating rates.

**5. Weak consumer pricing power: unit volumes up, ruble value flat or down, online prices falling** — A0128, A0409, A1345, A1261
- Market data: the large-appliance market is +7.4% in units but -0.6% in value. Small appliances are +17.6% in units and +13% in value. Average prices are falling, especially online. Consumers are trading down.
- Transmission: when FX cost savings appear, the market will force them into prices. Pass-through will be fast and margin upside limited.

**6. Marketplace channel economics shifting: Ozon gains share, rules and fees in flux** — A0586, A0853, A0443, A0768, A0293, A0597, A0594
- Ozon's share among large sellers rose from 42% to 58% (Jan→Aug). Part of the reason is drone damage to Wildberries logistics.
- Seller associations complained to FAS that new Ozon promo terms can force FBO stock to sell at a discount with no quantity cap. They also asked to amend the platform-economy law.
- Ozon introduces a PVZ (pickup-point) fee from 1 Dec.
- Transmission: margin leakage, inventory sold at discount, and concentration/receivable risk on one platform.

**7. Logistics and physical risk to inventory** — A0865, A1556, A0855, A0025
- Southern ports are almost stopped (−95–100% throughput, about 200 bn RUB per month in losses), and cargo is moving to the North-West and land crossings.
- A retailer (Krasnoe & Beloe) cut warehouse stock to 25–30% per delivery because of drone risk.
- Drone strikes on data centres are delaying arbitration courts.
- Transmission: freight cost and lead times, the case for spreading inventory across warehouses, and slower collections/litigation.

**8. Import cost and regulatory headwinds** — A1120, A1263, A1754, A0327, A0204, A1705
- The Minpromtorg head proposes raising duties to WTO-bound maximums on localized goods.
- A customs case revalued a Chinese-sourced cargo 3x by comparing it with a Western supplier's price.
- FNS (tax service) is focusing on "paper VAT."
- The finance bloc has been strengthened. The tax package is "unlikely" to be softened.
- Transmission: landed cost, customs-value disputes on Chinese-supplier invoices, and VAT-deduction counterparty risk.

## 2. Decisions

1. **Front-load yuan purchases and payment prepayments now.** Basis: A1570, A1342, A0311, A1327, A0618, A1081.
   - What: buy the CNY needed for the next 6–10 weeks of supplier payments (Nov–Dec, the pre-New-Year season) in tranches over 22–30 Oct, while the rate is around 12.2–12.3.
   - Why then: before the tax-period support fades and before the November talks risk.
   - Combines: the FX level, the tax-period seasonality, and the fragility of the geopolitics.
2. **Hedge the rest of the Q1 2027 yuan need.** Basis: A0037, A0115, A1682, A1848, A0765.
   - What: CNY/RUB forwards or futures for roughly 50% of Q1 needs at current levels.
   - Why: sanctions de-escalation is reversible (a revocable waiver, the APEC/November talks binary) and gives two-sided risk. Lock in, don't speculate.
3. **Hold refinancing; don't lock long fixed debt yet.** Basis: A1081, A1588, A1026, A0825, A1098, A0250.
   - Wait for the 23 Oct CBR decision.
   - Expect a hawkish hold, so keep the current structure.
   - When renewing working-capital lines, prefer short tenors or floating rates linked to the key rate. The CBR path implies easing in 2027, so fixing at today's ~16–17% for long tenors is costly.
   - Get bank quotes and benchmark them against key rate + ~150–180bp.
4. **Diversify payment banks and add a buffer.** Basis: A1592, A0800, A0584, A0765, A0662, A1999.
   - Keep at least two Russian banks with working CNY correspondent routes.
   - Test a second route this month.
   - Keep a 2–4-week CNY payment buffer on account in case a channel disruption hits before Chinese New Year.
5. **Pricing guidance to the commercial team.** Basis: A0128, A1570, A0409, A0586.
   - Pass the FX gain selectively: price-match online, where prices are falling, and hold shelf prices offline where the market allows.
   - Use the margin to absorb promo costs rather than leading price cuts.
   - Reprice weekly against the CNY rate.
6. **Marketplace exposure controls.** Basis: A0443, A0768, A0293, A0586, A0853.
   - Audit Ozon promo auto-enrolment on FBO stock now and cap the stock sent to FBO in promo-prone categories.
   - Budget the higher channel fees.
   - Keep Wildberries as an active second channel to avoid single-platform concentration.
   - Monitor receivable cycles.
7. **Inventory and logistics plan.** Basis: A0865, A1556, A0855.
   - Budget higher freight cost and longer lead times for the North-West/land routes.
   - Split peak-season stock across several warehouses to limit drone-loss concentration.
   - Review cargo insurance.
   - Note the trade-off: smaller, more frequent deliveries raise working-capital needs.
8. **Customs and tax hygiene.** Basis: A1263, A1754, A1120.
   - Get documentary support for contract prices on Chinese invoices, since customs revaluation risk is real.
   - Screen VAT counterparties.
   - Build a duty-increase scenario (+ several pp) into the 2027 budget for localized categories (appliances).

## 4. Traps
- **Daily official-rate stories (A1327, A1395, A0606)** — these repeat the same move; don't treat each one as new information. Treat the euro moves as irrelevant, since payments are in CNY.
- **"Peace is near" headlines (A1409, A1763, A0891, A1356)** — these drive sentiment, not cash flows. Don't stretch the FX view or skip the hedge because of them. Lukoil/OFAC license extensions (A1564, A1628) do not touch our channels.
- **Medvedev/Zakharova rhetoric (A0448, A0481, A0765 framing)** — noise. Only an actual listing matters.
- **Frozen assets in Euroclear (A1511, A1678), US debt (A0759), French yields (A1337)** — no transmission to the company.
- **Rate forecasts in mortgage stories (A0825)** — useful only as a reference for the CBR path, not a signal in themselves.
- **Tsifra broker's USD bond at 9.5% (A0242, A0407)** — not a realistic funding option for us. A USD liability would also add FX mismatch.
- **Tsifra/MTS bond specifics; non-public ratings law (A0554)** — at most a long-term option for a bond debut. Don't act on it now.
- **China GDP and polymer overcapacity (A0205, A0727)** — background only.
- **Quail eggs, outstaffing, black lenders (A1345, A0998, A0166)** — negligible for us.
- **The ~100% national-currency claim (A0800)** — does not mean payments are frictionless (see A0584).
