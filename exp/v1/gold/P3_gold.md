# P3 Gold Memo: General Counsel, mid-size marketplace + own brands on WB/Ozon

## 1. Key issues

1. **Seller associations are pushing hard for regulation of platform rules (promo terms, fees, ranking)**. Items: A0443, A0768, A1035 (with background in A0293, A0597).
   - **Path:** sellers complain to FAS and Minpromtorg about Ozon's forced-promo mechanics (A0443), and the Union of Marketplace Sellers asks the Presidential Administration, FAS and the Duma to amend the platform-economy law (A0768). The asks are fee unbundling, free choice of delivery method and a ban on pessimizing sellers in search results. If these succeed, our own marketplace's offer and fee structure become a target. As a seller on Ozon, the same fight gives us leverage.
   - **Why now:** two association letters went out within the same week (15–16 Oct). This is the window to shape the amendments before they are drafted.
2. **Consumer-protection pressure on marketplace dark patterns and advertising claims**. Items: A1035, A0182, A1187/A1261, A1979/A1913/A1825.
   - **Path:** the Consumers' Union calls out countdown timers and stock counters, and Rospotrebnadzor reports rising complaints, over 31.5k in 9 months in one region (A1035). FAS ruled MegaFon's ad misleading and is considering liability (A0182). This shows FAS is willing to judge whether an advertised feature actually exists. Meta's $25bn fine (A1979, A1913, A1825) was imposed under consumer-protection law for misleading privacy claims, not under data-protection law.
   - **Why now:** together these point to a regulator theory that misleading UX, ad claims and privacy statements are unfair practices. Our site promos, our own-brand ads and our privacy notice are all exposed.
3. **Critical PHP RCE vulnerabilities and the personal-data breach duty**. Items: A1730, A1902, A1507, A1449, A1495, A1460, A1803, A0478.
   - **Path:** tens of thousands of Runet sites are exposed, including those on 1C-Bitrix and WordPress (A1730). Patches were released on 21 Oct, but exploitation was already reported (A1507, A1460). Separately, the VMmanager hosting stack was breached (A0478), which is a vendor risk if our hosters use it.
   - **Why now:** if our storefront is PHP-based, any compromise of customer data triggers personal-data breach notification to the regulator (Roskomnadzor) and liability.
4. **Infrastructure outages from data-center strikes affect contracts, the state systems that compliance depends on, and courts**. Items: A0421, A0262, A0581/A0595, A0185, A0691, A0025, A0041.
   - **Path:** AKIT is proposing an emergency regime for trade when the state information systems (GIS) used for compliance fail (A0421). Yandex services, Yandex Direct ads and tenants hosted on Yandex were down (A0262, A0581, A0595). Avito and Google Cloud had provider outages (A0185, A0691). Arbitrazh hearings are being postponed (A0025). The temporary-management decree targets companies with poor drone protection (A0041).
   - **Why now:** we need to check force-majeure and SLA clauses, ad-spend reconciliation for the 11–14 Oct period, and procedural deadlines in pending cases.
5. **Marking and labeling expansion from 1 Nov**. Items: A1801, A0776.
   - **Path:** Chestny Znak (the national product-marking system) extends to vegetable oils, dairy and fertilizers, with marking codes required per package in universal transfer documents (UPD) (A1801). This affects our own-brand SKUs and third-party sellers on our marketplace. The Metro fine for unlabeled goods (A0776) shows active enforcement.
   - **Why now:** the deadline is 10 days away.
6. **Brand impersonation and fraud targeting marketplace customers**. Items: A0762, A0442, A0773/A0788, A0164, A0186.
   - **Path:** phishing most often imitates retail brands (39%, A0762). The fake "written-off stock" scheme hit RWB customers (A0442). The Interior Ministry (MVD) is warning about prepayment risks (A0773, A0788).
   - **Why now:** we need brand-protection takedowns, customer warnings, and to manage the liability and reputational risk of compromised accounts.
7. **Ozon's partner-contract moves and channel shift**. Items: A0293, A0597, A0586, A0853.
   - **Path:** Ozon has introduced an agent fee for pickup-point (PVZ) owners, citing the legal requirement that agency services be paid (A0293). Large sellers' sales are shifting toward Ozon (about 58%, A0586, A0853).
   - **Why now:** this is a reference point for our own PVZ and agency contracts. It also means our own-brand dependence on Ozon's terms is growing.
8. **Tax and labor compliance in e-commerce**. Items: A0998, A1754, A1471, A0600.
   - **Path:** the Federal Tax Service (FNS) outstaffing experiment already covers 35–40% of e-commerce (A0998), and the FNS is cracking down on "paper VAT" (A1754). Both are relevant to warehouse staffing contractors and the base of self-employed sellers.

## 2. Decisions

1. **Urgent PHP and vendor exposure check (this week).** Require IT/infosec to confirm by written memo that all systems are patched, and get written statements from our hosting providers about any VMmanager use. Prepare a Roskomnadzor breach-notification playbook covering the 24h and 72h notices. (A1730, A1902, A1507, A0478)
2. **Join or draft a lobbying position on the platform-economy law amendments (submit within 2–3 weeks).** We sit on both sides, so the position must be split. As a seller, support transparent fee unbundling and limits on forced promos. As an operator, oppose a blanket mandatory choice of delivery method and an undefined "pessimization" ban; propose clear objective ranking disclosures instead. Engage through AKIT and the sellers' associations. (A0768, A0443, A1035) *This combines several items.*
3. **Audit our own marketplace promo and offer rules before FAS looks at them (by end of November).** Make sure sellers can cap the quantity sold at a promo price and can opt out of promotions, and that fees are itemized. Amend the offer with the notice period the law requires. (A0443, A0768, A0293)
4. **Dark-pattern and advertising-claim review (30 days).** Remove or substantiate countdown timers and "X left" counters, own-brand ad claims and privacy-notice statements. Make sure each claim is factually true at the time it is shown. (A1035, A0182, A1979/A1913) *This combines several items.*
5. **Chestny Znak readiness for 1 Nov.** Confirm UPD-level marking codes for own-brand dairy and oil SKUs. Update seller rules to require marking for the new categories, with delisting and indemnity clauses. (A1801, A0776)
6. **Force-majeure, SLA and continuity contract review.** Review contracts with cloud, hosting and ad platforms, and claim reconciliation from Yandex Direct for 11–14 Oct. Docket all pending cases for hearing postponements and track procedural deadlines. Support the AKIT emergency-regime proposal. (A0421, A0581/A0595, A0262, A0691, A0025)
7. **Brand-protection program.** Monitor and take down phishing domains, publish warnings to customers, and add seller-verification rules that ban off-platform prepayment requests. (A0762, A0442, A0773)
8. **Review staffing contractors under the FNS outstaffing experiment.** Consider voluntary participation and run counterparty VAT due diligence. (A0998, A1754)

- Government response to the AKIT GIS emergency-regime letter (A0421).
- Rospotrebnadzor actions on marketplace manipulation (A1035), and FAS's final penalty on MegaFon (A0182).
- PHP exploitation reports in Runet, and the scope of the VMmanager breach (A1730, A0478).
- Further data-center strikes and court IT restoration (A0025, A0262), and criteria for the temporary-management decree (A0041).

- Self-employed income limit indexation (A1471).

## 4. Traps

- **Meta $25bn fine (A1979/A1913/A1825):** this is a US state-law consumer case. It works as a signal for privacy-claim hygiene, not as a measure of our Russian exposure.
- **European government site shutdowns (A1449, A1495, A1460, A1803):** these are duplicates of the PHP story. Do not shut down our own site out of copycat reaction; patch it instead.
- **Akmal/Lapina remix suit (A0498, A0916):** a celebrity dispute with only weak IP relevance.
- **OpenAI agent monitoring (A0775, A0820) and DeepSeek US ban (A0305):** foreign AI-governance news that does not create a near-term duty for us.
- **Constitutional Court ruling on assigning litigation costs (A0724):** marginal, though it slightly raises the risk of consumer lawsuits financed by lawyers.
- **ICC award recognition (A1386), sanctions news (A1999), external management of foreign companies (A0019):** cross-border context with no direct link to us.
- **WB state certificates (A0486), VTB/WB discount (A0594), digital ruble (A0447), WhatsApp audience (A0287), foreign brands (A0409), Minfin taxes (A0327), deepfake (A1276), regional-decision fines (A0405), AI-written lawsuits (A1623), Supreme Court ruling on photos (A1601):** commercial or background items that do not drive a legal decision this week. A1601 is useful only as a reminder about listing-image rights.
- **"Emotional shoppers" commentary (A1187/A1261):** an academic opinion piece, not regulatory action.
