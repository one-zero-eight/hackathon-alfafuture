# Memo U01: CFO, oil and gas (OKVED B), Siberia / Far East, credit and leasing

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Buy FX for near-term FX payables now.** Buy the next 1-3 months of FX-denominated payables in two or three tranches over the next 10 days. Cap new forward hedges at 30% of that need. Owner: Treasury. Items: A0618, A1395, A1327. Path: the official USD rate fell from 82.89 (21 Oct) to 81.97 (22 Oct), so FX bought now costs less in roubles. Check: rouble strengthening, so accelerate purchases and hedge less. Payables vs receivables: the brief does not say what currency your export receivables are in. If they are in USD or EUR, the stronger rouble reduces rouble revenue, so do not add FX sales hedges until this is confirmed.

2. **Fix part of the credit line now, for up to 2 years.** Fix about 40-50% of the next 12 months of credit needs. Keep the rest floating. Owner: CFO. Items: A1081, A1098. Path: the CBR meeting on 23 Oct is expected to hold rates with a hawkish tone, and the cut is not expected before 2027 (Shokhin). Inflation expectations are 13.2% (A1098). Check: rates stay high, so fix now. The playbook warns against long fixed debt when cuts are signalled, and no cut is signalled before 2027, so keep fixed tenors to 2 years or less.

3. **Re-base the budget to a Brent range and a higher freight line.** Run the budget at Brent around $108-111. Brent touched $111.05 on 20 Oct, then traded at $107.97 by 18:22 (A0453, A0649). Add the record Argus freight rate of $27-48/bbl from Novorossiysk and Primorsk, which widens discounts on Russian crude (A1485). Owner: CFO with commercial. Check: one-off vs persistent. Treat freight as persistent until contracts show who bears it (Incoterms).

4. **Build tax scenarios, not reserves.** Model the gas TRIZ tax benefit (A0337) and the 2027-2029 tax package (A0327) as scenarios only. Neither has an effective date or an approved text. Owner: tax lead. Check: effective date and applicability are not yet set.

5. **Check exposure to the proposed fuel exchange rules.** Government is studying limits on access to exchange trading for participants with a high "exchange index" (A1547). By 31 Oct, confirm whether any of your fuel sales would fall under this. Owner: Legal and Commercial. Check: applicability unknown, it is a proposal.

6. **Test one CNY payment route.** Run one test payment to China through a second bank. USD and EUR are not traded on the Moscow Exchange, so CNY is the listed reference (A1570). Mishustin says RF-China settlements are near 100% in national currencies (A0800). Owner: Treasury. Check: channel diversification, not a response to a stated stress.

## 2. Monitor

- **CBR decision on 23 Oct (A1081).** If the CBR signals a cut, stop further fixing and keep the floating share.
- **Brent back above $111 (A0453).** Re-run the budget downside case.
- **Russian crude discount to China (A0722).** Discount was 5.2% in August, against 15.6% in July. If it returns to the July level, cut the price assumption.
- **Rouble reversal (A0323).** If the official USD rate closes back above 83, stop the FX purchase tranches.

- **A0661 (European gas at a 2022 high).** Your contracts are not stated to be linked to European gas prices.
- **A1830, A1362, A1296 (rail and auto leasing, SSJ-100 engines).** Programme details for other sectors, no direct effect on your financing.
- **A0131 (current account surplus).** Macro data, no treasury or tax action.

Shortcut: fixing and FX buys assume rouble and rate moves hold for at least a month. Not checked: your actual FX mix and current debt maturities, which the brief does not give.
