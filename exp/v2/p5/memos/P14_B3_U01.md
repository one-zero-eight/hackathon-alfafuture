## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix rates on 40-60% of needed borrowing with tenor of 12 months or more, executed this week.** Driver: A1081 (CBR expected to hold on 23 Oct; cuts not before 2027). Transmission: no cut in sight, so floating debt keeps its current cost. Check (rule 1): rate expected to stay high, no cut signalled, so fixing is allowed.

2. **Confirm the net FX position for 1-3 month needs within 3 working days, then act on it.** Driver: A1342 (official USD 81.97 on 22 Oct, first move below 82 since 7 Aug), A1570 (ruble up four sessions in a row). Transmission: a stronger ruble makes FX payables cheaper now. Check (rule 2): strengthening means accelerate purchases for payables and hedge less. For receivables, the same move cuts ruble revenue, so do not pre-sell beyond the hedge floor.

3. **Hedge 30-60% of confirmed 1-3 month FX needs (payables side only), in tranches of one-third per week.** Driver: A1342, A1570. Owner: treasury with CFO sign-off. Check: direction of the move on payables is favourable, so the tranche sizes stay at the lower end of the band.

4. **Re-run the Q4 budget and price guidance at freight of $27-48 per barrel and Brent around $111.05.** Driver: A1485 (record Argus freight from Novorossiysk and Primorsk; wider discounts), A0453 (Brent above $111 for the first time in five months). Check (rule 3): one-off or persistent? The items tie freight to shipping risk and low stocks but give no end date, so treat it as persistent for a six-month scenario.

5. **Confirm by 31 Oct whether any facility is a hazardous object under OSOPO, then price the new tariff.** Driver: A1661 (new tariffs from 1 Nov 2026). Transmission: higher insurance cost, which should be reserved. Check (rule 4): effective date is 1 Nov 2026. Applicability to your facilities is not stated in the item, so this needs your confirmation.

6. **Before items 2, 3 and 5 are executed, confirm three facts with the user:** the net FX position, the hedge policy limit, and the hazardous-object status of facilities. These are not stated in the items and are not assumed here.
