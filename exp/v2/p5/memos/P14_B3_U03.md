# Memo U03: CEO / owner, retail and marketplaces (OKVED G), Moscow

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Assign an owner to the 1 Nov 2026 labelling extension and finish a scope check by 31 Oct.** Driver: A1801 (Chestny Znak marking extends to vegetable oils, dairy and fertilisers from 1 Nov 2026). Transmission: unmarked stock in those categories cannot be sold once the rule applies. Check (rule 1): does the change apply to your OKVED G assortment and from which date? Date is 1 Nov 2026. Assortment is not in the items, so confirm before buying stock.

2. **Audit the return policy against the updated no-return list before the next promotion cycle.** Driver: A1941 (updated retail rules from 1 Sep 2026: medicines, hygiene goods, perfume, cosmetics, fabrics and cables cannot be returned without defect). Transmission: a policy that offers returns on these items creates refund cost and dispute risk. Check (rule 1): effective date and whether the company's categories are covered.

3. **Test one seasonal promotion cut on marketplace fees with a 2-week window.** Driver: A0594 (WB card discount up to 6% for VTB card holders). Transmission: a bank-linked discount pulls card-holder demand to your listings if you join the scheme. Check (rule 3): direction of the effect on your customers. Only the WB scheme is in the items, so this needs your check of whether your store is on WB.

4. **Confirm the platform fee position before renewing any marketplace contract.** Driver: A0768 (seller union asks to change the platform economy law to split fees for placement, logistics and advertising). Transmission: if the law changes, the fee structure of current contracts may change. Check (rule 1): this is a request, not a change in force, so the action is a contract-review step, not a compliance plan.

5. **Keep stock and cash plans at current levels; no capacity change.** Driver: A1050 (mortgage demand up in big cities), A0728 (office lease share up to 62%). Check (rule 3): neither item shows a change in your customers' spending on goods. No stock change is justified by these items.
