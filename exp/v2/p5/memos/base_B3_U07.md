# CFO memo: news week of 18-22 Oct 2026

Company: OKVED H road freight and logistics, Siberia/Far East; uses credit and leasing. Parameters marked "proposed" are my suggested thresholds, not facts from the items.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix only the 12-month share of new borrowing; keep anything longer floating.** Fix 50% (proposed) of debt needs for the next 12 months after the 23 Oct decision. Owner: Treasury, by 31 Oct. Drivers: A1081, A1098. Path: the CBR holds with a hawkish tone and cuts are not expected before 2027, so rates stay high near term. Check: rule 1 says fix when rates stay high, but do not lock long fixed debt while cuts are signalled. Applied: fix the near-term need only.

2. **Bring forward FX purchases for 1-3 month payables; hedge at the low end.** Buy USD/CNY for imported parts and leasing payments due in 1-3 months now, and cover 30% (proposed) by forward. Owner: Treasury, this week. Drivers: A1327, A1342, A1570, A0618. Path: USD fell to 81.97 and CNY to about 12.23, lowering the ruble cost of FX payables. Check: direction favours FX payables; ruble receivables are unaffected. A0618 quotes experts split between temporary and lasting moves, so no hedge above 30% until the trend holds.

3. **Rebuild the fuel budget with a scenario and add fuel clauses.** Run a fuel-cost scenario in the budget and cash forecast; add fuel-price indexation to new and renewed contracts. Owner: FP&A, by 5 Nov. Drivers: A0453, A1485, A0123. Path: Brent above $111 and record tanker freight raise diesel exposure. Check: one-off vs persistent. Brent already fell back from its peak (A0649), so the budget should be scenario-based, not a single fixed uplift.

4. **Raise price guidance on Black Sea-affected routes.** Raise quotes on transit and cross-border routes by a proposed 5-10% if we run them. Owner: Sales/pricing, by 31 Oct. Drivers: A0123, A0865. Path: Black Sea closure doubles Turkey-Iran transit cost and cuts port throughput. Check: persistent, since the closure is ongoing, not a one-off.

5. **Shorten credit terms for counterparties in exposed hubs.** Cap terms at 30 days (proposed) for customers operating through drone-hit ports or warehouses. Owner: Credit control, by 31 Oct. Drivers: A0865, A0855, A0658. Path: warehouses cut receiving to 25-30% of normal volume, so customer cash flow weakens. Check: actual exposure is unverified; first screen our customer list and apply only to confirmed names.

6. **Confirm the 1 Nov 2026 regulatory changes.** Check whether the laws effective 1 Nov (A1801) apply to our size and OKVED, quantify cash impact, and reserve if applicable. Owner: Legal/tax, by 30 Oct. Check: effective date is fixed; applicability is not stated in the card, so this is unverified.

## 2. Monitor

- **A1081, 23 Oct CBR decision.** If the decision is a cut or signals one in 2026, move unfixed debt to floating and stop fixing further.
- **A1327, A1570 ruble.** If USD returns above 83 (the level of A0323) for five trading days, reduce FX purchases and raise the hedge toward 60%.
- **A0346 diesel import damper.** If its cancellation is formally decided, re-run the fuel budget at once.
- **A1362, A1830 preferential auto leasing for 2027.** If trucks are confirmed as eligible in the 2027-2029 budget, review the timing of new leases.
