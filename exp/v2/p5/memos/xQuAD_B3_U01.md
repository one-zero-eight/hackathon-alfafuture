# Memo U01: CFO, OKVED B (oil and gas), Siberia / Far East, credit and leasing

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix the rate on about 50% of planned rouble borrowing for the next 12 months. Keep the rest floating. No fixed tenor beyond 2 years.** Items: A1081, A0825, A1098, A1060. Path: no key-rate cut expected before 2027, so floating debt stays expensive. Check: rate stays high, so rule 1 says fix. The cut view is one forecast (RSPP head), not central bank guidance, so tenor stays short. Owner: CFO and treasury.
2. **Buy FX now for the next 1-3 months of FX payables, up to 60% of that need.** Items: A1570, A1342, A0618, A1327. Path: the rouble is strong (official USD 81.97 for 22 Oct, lowest since 7 Aug; EUR 91.93). Check: a stronger rouble makes FX payables cheaper, so buy now. Net FX position is not in the cards; confirm it before execution. Owner: treasury.
3. **Rebuild the budget and cash forecast on three Brent cases: $95, $108 (A0649), and $111+ (A0453).** Add freight at $27-48/bbl from Novorossiysk and Primorsk as a cost (A1485). Trigger: if freight stays at record for two monthly cycles, update price guidance. Owner: FP&A. Check: one-off or persistent. Hormuz disruption (A0680, A1403) points to persistence, but the China discount narrowed to 5.2% (A0722). Use the scenario range; do not assume a direction.
4. **Confirm by 30 Oct which sites are hazardous production facilities, then quantify and reserve the OSOPO tariff change effective 1 Nov 2026.** Items: A1661. Owner: chief accountant. Check: effective date is stated; applicability to our sites is not in the card.
5. **Add a freight pass-through clause, indexed to a freight benchmark, to new export contracts.** Items: A1485, A0722. Path: freight and discount widening compress netbacks. Check: the clause is only useful if freight stays high, so apply to contracts longer than one quarter. Owner: commercial.
6. **Ask our two main banks by 15 Nov for their device-check timetable under the 1 March rule.** Items: A1043, A1071. Path: banks must refuse transfers from infected devices. Check: the duty falls on banks; our exposure is mobile and web payment flows only. Owner: IT and treasury.

## 2. Monitor

- **A1081, 23 Oct rate decision.** If the bank signals a cut, move the rest of planned borrowing to floating or short tenor.
- **A1570, A1342, ruble.** If the official USD falls below 80 or moves more than 3% in a week, raise FX hedging toward 60% of 1-3 month needs.
- **A0453, A0680, Brent and Hormuz.** If Brent holds above $111 or another tanker incident occurs, switch to the $111 budget case.
- **A1547, oil product exchange access.** If access limits are adopted, review our exchange sales route.
- **A0337 (gas TRIZ tax benefits) and A0327 (2027-29 tax package).** Act only if a draft changes our tax base.
