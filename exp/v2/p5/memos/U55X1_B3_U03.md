# Memo U03: CEO, retail and marketplaces (OKVED G), Moscow, credit and leasing

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Pause new Ozon FBO promotion sign-ups until the quantity cap works.** Owner: CEO with marketplace head. Items: A0443. Path: sellers say Ozon's new algorithms removed their ability to cap discounted quantities and make it harder to exit promotions. FBO sellers face the most risk. Check: the promotion change is a complaint filed with the FAS, not a ruling. Confirm whether we run FBO promotions at all. If not, no action.

2. **Rebalance stock on your own Ozon and Wildberries data, not on the market share.** Owner: CEO with category leads. Items: A0853, A0586. Path: Ozon's share of large-seller sales rose to about 60% after drone strikes on Wildberries logistics (MPStats), but Wildberries says the figures miss the full picture. Check: demand direction for your customers. Use two weeks of your own sales before moving stock.

3. **Map the 1 November marking and alcohol rules to your SKUs now.** Owner: CEO assigns, deadline 1 November. Items: A1801. Path: from 1 November, the marking system extends to vegetable oils, dairy and fertilisers, and alcohol rules tighten. Check: effective date is 1 November 2026. Scope applies only if we sell those goods.

4. **Check the returns policy against the September list.** Owner: Legal and operations. Items: A1941. Path: from 1 September, items such as medicines, hygiene products, perfume, cosmetics, fabrics and cables cannot be returned without a defect. Check: already in force per the card. Confirm our policy and refund flow match it.

5. **Remove or verify countdown timers and "only N left" counters.** Owner: marketing. Items: A1035. Path: the consumer union says these practices are manipulative. Check: applies only if we use such widgets. Cheap and reversible.

6. **Confirm warehouse continuity for drone-risk areas.** Owner: operations. Items: A0855, A0658. Path: Red&White cut its intake to 25-30% of its usual volume and reduced deliveries because of drone risk to its warehouses. Check: the cards do not say our region is affected. Confirm our warehouse locations, insurance and a backup fulfilment partner by 31 October.

7. **If we run pickup points, budget the Ozon fee.** Owner: CFO. Items: A0293, A0597. Path: from 1 December, Ozon charges pickup-point owners up to 1% of point revenue, or 1 rouble for points under one year old. Check: applies only to pickup-point owners.

8. **Shift appliance stock toward lower-priced SKUs, if we sell appliances.** Owner: category lead. Items: A0128. Path: large-appliance value fell 0.6% while units rose 7.4%. Small-appliance units rose 17.6%, value 13%. Check: our category mix.
