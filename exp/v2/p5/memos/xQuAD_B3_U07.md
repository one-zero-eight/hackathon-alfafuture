# Memo U07: CFO, OKVED H (freight and logistics), Siberia / Far East, credit and leasing

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix the rate on about 50% of the rouble borrowing needed in the next 12 months, now. Keep the rest floating, with no fixed tenor beyond 2 years.** Items: A1081 (no key-rate cut before 2027), A0825 (mortgage at 10% not before 2028), A1098 (inflation expectations 13.2%). Path: rates stay high, so floating debt stays expensive. Check: rule 1 says fix when rates stay high; the cut view is one forecast, so keep tenor short. Owner: CFO and treasury.
2. **Accelerate FX purchases for FX payables due in 1-3 months, up to 60% coverage. Hedge less on FX receivables.** Items: A1342 (official USD 81.97 on 22 Oct), A1395 (EUR 91.93), A0618 (rouble strengthening after the diesel deal), A1570 (fourth day of rouble gains). Check: strengthening rouble makes FX payables cheaper; confirm our net FX position, which the cards do not show. Owner: treasury.
3. **Rebuild the budget with diesel and freight scenarios.** Items: A0649 (Brent about $108), A0453 (Brent above $111), A1485 (tanker freight at record levels), A0123 (haulage demand up 20%). Trigger: if diesel rises for two consecutive months, update price guidance and add a fuel index to tariffs. Owner: FP&A. Check: one-off or persistent: Hormuz tension (A0680, A1403) points to persistence; treat as a 1-2 quarter cost.
4. **Audit driver licences and the paper trail for drivers now, before the proposed fines take effect.** Items: A0751 (proposal to raise fines for driving without a licence to 15,000-30,000 rub; repeat offences 30,000-50,000 rub). Owner: HR and operations. Check: a draft, not yet law; the audit is cheap and reversible.
5. **Confirm whether any port we use is operating.** Items: A0865 (southern port throughput stopped), A0126 (Baltic coal shipments may fall 80% in October). Owner: operations. Check: our corridors are not named in the cards.
