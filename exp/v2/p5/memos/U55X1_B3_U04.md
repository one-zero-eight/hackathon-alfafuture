# Memo U04: CEO, retail and marketplaces (OKVED G), Volga region, credit and leasing

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Assign an owner for the 1 November rules and set a 25 October check.** Owner: CEO assigns a compliance lead. Items: A1801. Path: from 1 November, the marking system extends to vegetable oils, dairy and fertilisers, and alcohol rules tighten. Tax administration changes too. Check: effective 1 November 2026. Scope applies only to the goods we sell.

2. **Pause FBO promotions on Ozon where we cannot cap quantities.** Owner: marketplace head. Items: A0443. Path: sellers report losing the ability to cap discounted quantity and to remove stock from sales, which hits FBO sellers hardest. Check: complaint to the FAS, not a ruling.

3. **Reallocate stock from Wildberries to Ozon only on two weeks of our own data.** Owner: CEO with category leads. Items: A0853, A0586. Path: Ozon's share of large-seller sales rose to about 60% by late summer after drone attacks on Wildberries logistics. Check: the figures are MPStats estimates, and Wildberries disputes them.

4. **Remove urgency timers and stock counters where they are not accurate.** Owner: marketing. Items: A1035. Path: the consumer union flags countdown timers and quantity counters as manipulative. Check: applies only if we use them.

5. **Budget the Ozon pickup-point fee if we own points.** Owner: CFO. Items: A0293, A0597. Path: from 1 December, pickup-point owners pay up to 1% of point revenue. Check: applies only to point owners. Ozon says it will spend over 10 bn roubles supporting owners in 2026 (A0597).

6. **Review appliance stock toward lower prices, if we sell appliances.** Owner: category lead. Items: A0128. Path: units up 7.4% while value fell 0.6% in large appliances. Check: our mix.

7. **Review the regional grocery franchise signal before any change to the franchise model.** Owner: CEO. Items: A0233. Path: franchise stores in regional grocery networks fell 2.3% this year, against overall market growth. Check: only relevant if we run or supply franchises. No action yet.
