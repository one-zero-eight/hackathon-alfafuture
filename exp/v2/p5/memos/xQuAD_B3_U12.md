# Memo U12: CFO, OKVED K (leasing and microfinance), Moscow, FX and deposits

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix the rate on about 50% of the funding needed for the next 12 months. Keep the rest floating, with no fixed tenor beyond 2 years.** Items: A1081 (no cut before 2027), A0825 (mortgage at 10% not before 2028), A1098 (inflation expectations 13.2%), A1060 (maximum deposit rate 13.06%). Path: rates stay high, so funding costs stay high. Check: rule 1 says fix when rates stay high; the cut outlook is one forecast, so tenor stays short. Owner: CFO and treasury.
2. **Size the funding plan on two growth cases: 15% (company guidance) and 30% (market pace).** Items: A0726 (Europlan new business up 30% in nine months to 90.8 bn rub; the company holds its 15% forecast). Path: competitors with integrated bank platforms grow faster. Check: part of Europlan's growth comes from integration with Alfa-Bank's retail leasing (A0726), which we do not share; plan the base case at 15%. Owner: CFO.
3. **Accelerate FX purchases for FX payables due in 1-3 months and hedge less on FX receivables.** Items: A0618, A1570, A1342, A0311, A1327. Check: direction of the rouble matters for payables versus receivables; confirm our net FX position, not shown in the cards. Owner: treasury.
4. **Ask two banks for quotes on a 6-12 month bond or bank tranche, to diversify funding.** Items: A0700 (corporate bonds reached a record 31% of corporate funding in 2026). Check: this is one funding option among others; choose on cost, not on the headline share. Owner: treasury.
5. **Keep deposits short.** Items: A1060, A1081. Check: no hike signalled, so a small partial lock is possible; default is short.
