# Memo U09: CEO / owner, OKVED C (food production), Volga region

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Confirm marking readiness for vegetable oils, dairy and fertilizers before 1 Nov, and alcohol if we make it.** Owner: CEO with operations, by 31 Oct. Items: A1801 (marking expansion to vegetable oils, dairy and fertilizers; tighter alcohol rules from 1 Nov). Check: effective date 1 Nov; applies only if our SKUs fall in these categories. Confirm.
2. **Hold consumer price increases for now.** Owner: pricing lead. Items: A1098 (inflation expectations down 1pp to 13.2%), A1081 (no rate cut before 2027, so credit-driven demand stays weak). Check: direction of effect on our buyers: slowing expectations argue against a price rise.
3. **Delay capex that needs new credit until after the 23 Oct CBR decision.** Owner: CFO and CEO. Items: A1081, A0825 (mortgage rates near 10% only if key rate falls to 8%, not before 2028). Check: no cut before 2027 means borrowing stays costly; postpone rather than cancel.
