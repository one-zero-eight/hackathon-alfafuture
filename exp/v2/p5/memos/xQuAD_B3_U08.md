# Memo U08: CFO, OKVED H (freight and logistics), Moscow, credit and leasing

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix the rate on about 50% of rouble borrowing needed in the next 12 months. Keep the rest floating, with no fixed tenor beyond 2 years.** Items: A1081 (no cut before 2027), A0825, A1098. Path: rates stay high. Check: rule 1 says fix when rates stay high; the cut outlook is one forecast, so keep tenor short. Owner: CFO and treasury.
2. **Accelerate FX purchases for payables due in 1-3 months, up to 60% coverage, and hedge less on receivables.** Items: A1570, A1395, A0618, A0311 (official and off-exchange USD at the lowest in about two months). Check: stronger rouble makes FX payables cheaper; confirm our net FX position first. Owner: treasury.
3. **Add a fuel and freight pass-through clause to contracts at renewal.** Items: A0123 (sharp price swings in haulage; two-fold transit cost rise via Iran after the Black Sea closure), A1485 (tanker freight at record levels), A0649 (Brent about $108). Owner: commercial. Check: fuel rises look persistent while Hormuz tension lasts; use a monthly index, not a one-off surcharge.
4. **Quote small-batch delivery prices for Krasnoe & Beloe if we serve them.** Items: A0855 and A0658 (the chain now takes 25-30% of its previous volume per delivery and cut per-delivery volume by more than 60%). Check: confirm whether we deliver to this chain. If we do, more frequent smaller trips raise our cost per tonne. Owner: operations.
5. **Check whether we carry cargo through any southern port or terminal now affected by drone strikes.** Items: A0865 (port throughput stopped; losses above 200 bn rub a month). Owner: operations. Check: exposure unknown from the cards.
6. **Get two truck-leasing quotes against our current terms by 15 Nov.** Items: A0726 (leasing market is competitive; Europlan new business up 30%). Owner: CFO. Threshold: switch if a quote beats current terms by more than 1 point.
