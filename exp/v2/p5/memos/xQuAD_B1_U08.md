# Memo U08: CFO, OKVED H (freight and logistics), Moscow, bank products credit and leasing

## Themes
- Rate path: CBR hold with no cut before 2027 (A1081); EUR official rate cut to 91.93 (A1395).
- Ruble strength: explained by a diesel deal, or temporary, per expert view (A0618); USD off-exchange below 82.5 (A0311).
- Fuel and freight: road freight demand up 20% in Q3 with a fuel crisis (A0123); record tanker freight and widening discounts (A1485); coal shift away from Baltic ports (A0126).
- Smaller, more frequent loads: a major retailer cuts stock per warehouse to 25-30% of prior volume after drone threats (A0855).
- Vehicle leasing: state funding for subsidised auto leasing may rise in the 2027 budget second reading (A1362).
- Deposits: maximum ceiling 13.06% (A1060), not a logistics lever.

## 1. Decisions now
1. **Fix the rate on 50-70% of floating credit and leasing debt due in the next 12 months, before or at the 23 Oct meeting.** Owner: CFO. Item: A1081. Path: no cut before 2027 keeps floating cost high. Check: rule 1 fixes when rates stay high; no cut is signalled. Caveat: a single forecast.
2. **Raise the cost-per-trip assumption and add a partial-load surcharge to price guidance.** Owner: FP&A and commercial. Items: A0855, A0123. Path: smaller, more frequent loads mean more trips per tonne. Check: the retailer's change is described as a test, so model it as a scenario first, then as persistent if repeated.
3. **Confirm the company's FX position, then accelerate purchases of 1-3 month FX payables at the low end of the 30-60% band.** Owner: Treasury. Items: A0618, A0311, A1395. Path: a stronger ruble cuts the rouble cost of FX-priced parts. Check: rule 2 assumes FX payables; do not execute until confirmed.
4. **Review fuel-price pass-through in customer contracts within 2 weeks.** Owner: commercial director. Items: A0123, A1485. Path: fuel and freight costs squeeze margin unless contracts index them. Check: persistent or one-off; add clauses but do not reprice yet.
5. **Request vehicle leasing quotes with and without the subsidy, ready to switch when the subsidy is funded.** Owner: CFO. Item: A1362. Check: the funding is still a proposal in the second reading.
6. **Set a monthly freight-rate watch against the budget.** Owner: FP&A. Item: A1485. Check: whether the watch reveals a sustained rise; reversible and cheap.

## 2. Monitor
- CBR statement on 23 Oct (A1081): if a cut is signalled, shorten the fixed share in decision 1.
- Subsidised auto leasing (A1362): if the second reading adds funds, apply for fleet leasing under the scheme.
- Freight market (A1485, A0123): if rates fall for four weeks, re-check the surcharge in decision 2.
- Ruble (A0618, A0311): if the USD move reverses for three sessions, return FX purchases to base.
