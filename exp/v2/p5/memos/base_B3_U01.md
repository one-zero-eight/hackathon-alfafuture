# Memo U01: CFO, oil and gas (OKVED B), Siberia / Far East, credit and leasing

Assumptions: debt maturities, FX mix of payables, and who pays freight (Incoterms) are not in the items. Confirm before acting.

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix the rate on the next 12 months of credit need, up to 50% of that tranche. Keep the rest floating.** Owner: CFO with bank lead. Items: A1081, A1098. Path: the CB holds on 23 Oct with a hawkish tone, so near-term rates stay high. Check (rule 1): no cut is signalled by the CB; the "no cut before 2027" view (A1081) is one RSPP forecast. No fixing beyond 12 months.

2. **Buy FX for 1-3 month import and capex payables now, in tranches: about 40% this week, the rest over four weeks.** Owner: treasury. Items: A0618, A1570, A1395 (official USD 81.97 and EUR 91.93 on 22 Oct; ruble up four sessions). Path: a stronger ruble makes purchases cheaper. Check (rule 2): direction favours payables, so accelerate.

3. **Hold the export-receivable hedge at its current level. Add no new sell hedges in this move.** Owner: CFO. Items: A0618, A0649. Path: a stronger ruble lowers the ruble value of exports. Check (rule 2): the playbook says "hedge less" when strengthening, but that rule is written for FX needs. For receivables the logic points the other way. This is a conflict; you choose which way to lean.

4. **Rebuild the budget and cash forecast with two cases: Brent at $100 and at $111; freight at $27-48 per barrel.** Owner: FP&A. Items: A0453, A0649, A1485. Path: record freight widens discounts on Novorossiysk and Primorsk barrels. Check (rule 3): treat freight as persistent for 2027, since low Hormuz stocks are cited (A1485). Treat the Brent spike as one-off until it holds for four weeks.

5. **Test a second export route and a second CNY settlement bank with a small share of volume.** Owner: logistics and treasury. Items: A0865 (southern ports idle, losses above 200 bn RUB a month), A0800 (Russia-China settlement near 100% in national currencies). Path: if volume depends on southern ports, that route is at risk. Check (rule 5): actual exposure unknown; confirm the share of volume through Novorossiysk, Taman or Tuapse.

6. **Prepare a benefit file for the gas TRIZ incentives proposed by Minfin.** Owner: tax director. Items: A0337. Path: benefits for hard-to-recover gas reserves are with agencies for approval. Check (rule 4): not law yet and effective date unknown. Prepare the file; book nothing.

## 2. Monitor

- CB decision on 23 Oct (A1081). Trigger: any cut signal, then move part of the 12-month fixed need to floating.
- Official USD rate (A0323, A0606). Trigger: back above 83, then slow the tranche purchases.
- Brent (A0649, A0453). Trigger: a week below $100, then cut the budget case.
- Freight and China discount (A1485, A0722: 5.2% in August, 15.6% in July). Trigger: discount back above 15% or freight above $48, then review price guidance.
- US "hell sanctions" freeze (A0037). Trigger: reactivation, then review FX payment channels.
