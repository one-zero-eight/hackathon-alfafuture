# Memo U12: CFO, financial services (leasing and microfinance, OKVED K), Moscow

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix the rate on 40-60% of needed funding of 12 months or more, executed this week.** Driver: A1081 (CBR expected to hold on 23 Oct; cuts not before 2027), A1060 (maximum deposit rate 13.06%). Transmission: a leasing book funded at floating rates loses margin if rates stay high. Check (rule 1): rate expected to stay high and no cut signalled, so fixing is allowed.

2. **Hedge 30-60% of confirmed 1-3 month FX needs, payables side only, this week.** Driver: A1342 (USD official 81.97 on 22 Oct), A0323 and A0606 (USD below 83 and 82 in the past days). Transmission: a stronger ruble lowers the ruble cost of FX funding and imported equipment. Check (rule 2): strengthening means accelerate purchases and hedge less. The net FX position is not stated, so confirm payables versus receivables first.

3. **Re-price new leases on a fixed-rate basis, with a rate reset clause.** Driver: A0726 (Europlan new business up 30% to 90.8 bn roubles after merging with Alfa retail leasing). Transmission: higher lease volume at unchanged cost means the pricing has to stay tight. Check (rule 3): one-off or persistent? The growth is a company-level result, not a shock, so keep the price tied to the funding cost.

4. **Confirm the 1 Nov 2026 insurance tariff effect on any hazardous-object assets held as collateral or leased.** Driver: A1661 (OSOPO tariffs from 1 Nov 2026). Check (rule 4): applicability to your leased assets is not stated; confirm before reserving.

5. **Ask the user for the leasing book's exposure to marketplace sellers before tightening any credit terms.** Driver: none in the items for this company. Check (rule 5): actual exposure is not in the items; no tightening until it is known.
