# Memo U06: CEO, freight and logistics (OKVED H), Siberia / Far East, credit and leasing

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Add fuel indexation to new freight contracts within 2 weeks.** Owner: commercial director. Items: A0453, A1072, A1485. Path: Brent hit $111, a five-month high, on 20 Oct before easing (A0453), EU fuel prices rose 31.4% year on year in September (A1072), and freight rates for Russian crude hit records (A1485). Check: one-off vs persistent. Brent has already eased, but fuel prices have risen for three months. Treat as persistent and add an index clause rather than cutting prices.

2. **Freeze new truck purchases until Q4 volumes are known.** Owner: CEO. Items: A0123. Path: Q3 road freight demand rose 20%, but expert reports show cargo shifting from road to rail because of price swings. Check: demand direction for our customers. If our customers move to rail, road volume falls. Short-term leasing is reversible, so prefer it to purchases.

3. **Check whether the Kazakhstan border is on our routes, and add buffer days.** Owner: operations. Items: A0123. Path: China deliveries have become more expensive because of border crossing problems with Kazakhstan. Check: the card does not say whether our routes cross there. If they do, plan 1-2 extra days for border crossings.

4. **Confirm whether the auto-leasing programme covers trucks before signing 2027 leases.** Owner: CFO. Items: A1362, A1830. Path: the government plans to expand subsidised auto-leasing for 2027, pending the second reading of the budget. Check: the cards describe programmes for cars and rail rolling stock, not trucks. Do not assume subsidies apply.
