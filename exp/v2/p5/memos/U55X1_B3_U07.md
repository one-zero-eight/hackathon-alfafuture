# Memo U07: CFO, freight and logistics (OKVED H), Siberia / Far East, credit and leasing

## 1. Decisions now

1. **Fix about 40% of the next 12 months of credit and lease rates, up to 2 years.** Owner: CFO. Items: A1081, A1327. Path: the CBR is expected to hold on 23 October with a hawkish tone, and the first cut is not expected before 2027 (A1081). Check: rates stay high, so fix now. Keep tenors to 2 years or less, since no cut is signalled before 2027.

2. **Buy FX for 1-3 month FX costs now, in tranches.** Owner: Treasury. Items: A1327, A1395, A0618. Path: the official USD rate fell to 81.97 on 22 October, and the rouble has strengthened four days running. Check: the playbook says strengthening means accelerate FX purchases and hedge less. Confirm which of your costs are in FX (fuel imports, parts, leases) against your rouble freight revenue. Do not add hedges above 30% of the need.

3. **Index fuel costs in new contracts, and re-base the budget.** Owner: CFO with commercial. Items: A0453, A1072, A1485. Path: Brent reached $111 before easing; EU fuel prices rose 31.4% year on year; Russian crude freight hit records. Check: one-off vs persistent. Treat the fuel rise as persistent until the spot price settles, and add an index clause.

4. **Quantify the driving-licence fine rise and reserve for the scenario.** Owner: legal with HR. Items: A0751. Path: if we employ drivers without valid licences, the proposed fine rises to 15,000-30,000 roubles, with 30,000-50,000 for repeat offences. Check: the draft has been approved by the government commission but is not law. Scenario only, no reserve yet.

5. **Confirm whether subsidised leasing covers trucks.** Owner: CFO. Items: A1362, A1830. Path: the 2027 budget may expand subsidised auto-leasing and a rail-stock trade-in scheme. Check: the cards describe cars and rail stock, not trucks. Ask the lessor before signing.
