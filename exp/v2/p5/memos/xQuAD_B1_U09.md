# Memo U09: CEO / owner, OKVED C (food production), Volga region, bank products credit and leasing

## Themes
- Labelling: marking expansion from 1 Nov to vegetable oils, dairy and fertilisers, plus tighter alcohol rules (A1801).
- Utility costs: regional utility tariff indexation from 1 Oct, national average about 9.9% (A0575). EU fuel prices up 31.4% y/y (A1072) is context only.
- Customs and inputs: a pesticide producer contests a threefold customs valuation of imported raw material (A1263).
- Metrology fee: a planned fee on registering measuring instruments, higher for foreign equipment (A0721).
- Leasing: Europlan new business up 30%, a sign of competitive leasing terms (A0726).
- No effect in the packet: rail programme (A1830), sanctions rhetoric (A0765).

## 1. Decisions now
1. **Assign a compliance owner, check SKUs and run a test batch through the marking system before 1 Nov.** Owner: compliance lead. Item: A1801. Path: vegetable oils and dairy move into marking from 1 Nov. Check: whether the company's products fall in scope; the packet does not state it. If yes, this is a hard deadline.
2. **Review indexation clauses in utility-linked and supply contracts, and reprice where the clause allows.** Owner: CFO and commercial director. Item: A0575. Path: tariff increases from 1 Oct raise the cost base. Check: regulated tariffs rise annually, so treat as persistent. EU fuel data (A1072) is not a Russian cost signal.
3. **Update food-product price guidance for the utility increase.** Owner: commercial director. Item: A0575. Path: rule 3 updates price guidance when costs rise. Check: persistent, as in decision 2.
4. **Review the customs valuation of imported inputs against contract prices and declarations.** Owner: procurement with accounting. Item: A1263. Check: whether the company imports raw materials at all; the packet does not say. Act only if it does.
5. **Request leasing quotes from several providers before any leasing renewal.** Owner: CFO. Item: A0726. Path: competitive leasing terms are available. Check: whether any lease is due; not stated.
6. **Confirm the measuring-instrument register so the fee impact can be sized once adopted.** Owner: quality manager. Item: A0721. Check: whether any instruments are foreign; not stated.
